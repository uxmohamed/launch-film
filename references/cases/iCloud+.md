# iCloud+ (Apple service spot) — 57 frames ≈ 28.5 s (content #1–54 ≈ 27 s)

## 1. One-line concept
A short Apple service spot. The glossy iCloud cloud breaks into bubbles, and each bubble family carries one benefit: "Get more storage / More memories. / More peace of mind. / For more sharing. / That's a plus." Then the bubbles re-assemble into the cloud. It is a bookended **particle lineage**, where the logo becomes the particles and the particles become the logo.

## 2. Visual world
- **Ground:** a near-white cool grey (#F3F3F4) with no texture. There is soft ambient blue light around the hero objects.
- **Atom:** a glossy 3D blue sphere (the cloud's lobes). It is subsurface-lit, with a specular highlight and a soft cyan glow. Every later object is a sphere: photo bubbles, app-icon tokens, Memoji avatar balls in pastel spheres.
- **Type:** SF Pro Display Semibold headlines at about 4.5% h, with a two-line Regular subtitle at about 1.6% h below. They are centred, black.
- **Arrival state:** each headline arrives as **cyan glowing, blurred text** (a bloom of about 15–25 px in the brand blue), then cools to solid black 1–2 frames later.
- **Depth:** a heavy depth of field. Spheres pass the lens at 3–4× size, very blurred (#12–13, #47). The background spheres are blurred 8–15 px. There is one real device, an iPhone 15 on a slow 3D turntable (#7–10).

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Logo burst into atoms** (#5→6): the cloud's lobes separate as spheres and reveal the phone. The cause is "iCloud holds your stuff": the cloud opens.
- **Device → photo trail** (#10→11): the photos stream out of the phone as a curling line of bubbles, a lineage from app content to free particles.
- **Particle family swap** (#21→22, #30→31, #42→43): each benefit's particles blur out while the next headline glows in. The particle *type* changes (photos → app tokens → Memoji), but the sphere form stays.
- **Rack-focus selection** (#38→40): depth of field picks one person out of the group, as in the manual's DoF selection.
- **Vortex collapse** (#42→43): all avatars blur and swirl to centre, emptying the stage for the tagline.
- **Reassembly into the logo** (#46→49): the spheres fly back and fuse into the cloud, the exact reverse of #6.
- **Ripple under the end card** (#50–51): a single concentric ring expands from the logo. It is the only "shout", and a quiet one.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- **Glow-cool arrival state:** every headline (#7, #14, #22, #32, #43, #49) appears first as a cyan bloom with white-ish letters, and 1–2 frames later is solid black. The brand colour is the *heat* of new text. It is a luminous variant of Veryfront's "hot text".
- The **subtitle is a slot reel**. At #39 the old line slides up and the new one enters beneath it, half-overlapped, with motion blur.
- The photo trail is a logarithmic spiral. The thumbnails scale by about 1.15× per step, so the curl reads as rushing toward the viewer.
- The app tokens are **3D-extruded glossy icons**, not flat. Apple restyles its own icons to match the sphere language.
- Memoji spheres are pastel-coded (orange, blue, pink, green, purple, yellow), and the selected one is iCloud blue.
- The "Get more storage" words sit **either side of the phone**, and the phone stands in for the gap between them (#7–9).
- The foreground bubble at #12 is blurred about 40 px and crosses the frame in 2 frames, giving a strong sense of lens depth at almost no cost.

## 6. Pacing curve
- The logo open takes 3 s. Each benefit then takes 4–4.5 s (headline glow-in by about 0.5 s, subtitle by about 1 s, a 2.5–3 s hold with drifting particles).
- There is a 2 s tagline beat, then a 1 s reassembly and a 3 s end-card hold.
- It is very even, with no climax other than the reassembly. Energy comes from the particle drift, not from the cuts. There are no hard cuts at all; every change is a blur/particle handoff.

## 7. What makes it premium
- One primitive (a glossy sphere) explains five benefits and both logo moments.
- Text is lit (the glow arrival) rather than animated in position, and it never slides.
- Depth-of-field and lens-passing foreground spheres give it a real-camera feel on a flat grey ground.
- The logo burst and the reassembly bookend the film exactly.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Glow-cool headline.** New text appears in the brand colour with a 15–25 px outer glow and slight blur, then cross-fades to solid black within 0.5–1 s. It does not move.
2. **Logo shatter / logo reassemble.** Split the mark into its constituent primitives (lobes, dots), fly them out radially to reveal the content, and at the end reverse the exact paths.
3. **Particle family per benefit.** Keep the particle form (a sphere) and swap its content (photos → icons → avatars) per benefit. Blur the outgoing family 0→15 px as the next headline glows in.
4. **Lens-pass sphere.** Once per scene, send one particle past the camera at 3–4× size, blurred about 40 px, in 2 frames. It gives cheap depth.
5. **Rack-focus pick.** Sharpen one member and scale it 1.3× while the others blur about 10 px, in sync with a subtitle slot roll that names the reason.
6. **Ripple end card.** One concentric ring expands from behind the logo at about 20% opacity over 1 s, then the card holds 2 s.
