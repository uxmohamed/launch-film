# Microsoft Copilot ("Copilot, reimagined"): analysis (193 frames, ≈96 s)

## 1. One-line concept
This is the redesign film for Microsoft 365 Copilot, framed as a **designer's manifesto**:

> "Our design brief was simple / to better serve / you… To offer / what you need / when you need it… To amplify / your abilities… Copilot, reimagined / made for everyone / designed for you"

The spine is *construction*. The logo is drawn on a keyline grid, and icons stack into a sidebar that expands into the app. The app then scales down to phone width, and the features are shown as work "moments". Closing cards assemble into a cluster, and the Microsoft squares converge into the logo.

## 2. Visual world
- **Ground 1, a light-filled architectural void.** Photoreal 3D renders of white plaster rooms: a leaning slab, a skylight shaft, a corner. Hard sunlight casts diagonal shadows with soft leaf dapple (#2–15, #124–125). It's the "brand world": calm, physical, daylight.
- **Ground 2, design-spec paper.** Off-white with **blue hairline column guides and grid lines** (#20–33, #48–53, #131–150). Blue measurement annotations (padding arrows, "24"-type redlines) appear on #51–53.
- **Ground 3, a soft grey studio sweep** (#41–47, #76–123, #154–187): a near-white gradient with a faint cloud-like light.
- **Type pairing (deliberate):**
  - a **light-weight humanist grotesk** (Segoe-like, Light) for most words;
  - a **high-contrast italic serif** for the emotional word ("*you*", "*everyone*", "*reimagined*", "*Thursday*", "*your abilities*", doc titles such as "*Weekly Update: Project Aperture*").
  - Headline type sits at ~3–4% of frame height; the one shout is "amplify" at ~25% (#126–127) and "Your *Thursday* Summary" at ~15% (#136–137).
- **Palette:** greyscale throughout. Colour comes only from content: Office app icons, the red/cream PowerPoint slide, the green "Protect your time" block, the gradient Copilot icon (#75), and photos.
- **UI staging:** frameless windows with a large radius and a soft shadow (~30 px blur, ~6%) on the grey sweep. Macro crops of single components (input, suggestion chips, model picker). Cards float in 3D depth with the background ones blurred (#89, #173–176).
- **Cursor:** a **solid black dot** (~1.2% of frame) instead of an arrow (#66, #76–91, #106–110).

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- **Type is lit by the world.** "*you*" sits exactly inside the skylight's light patch (#12–15). Headline opacity follows the light: it fades out as the frame overexposes (#5, #11).
- **Two-tone phrase.** "Our design brief" is darker than "was simple" (#3–4), and the lighter half fades first.
- **The keyline grid is the icon's own construction grid** (#24–27): square keylines and circle guides, as in an icon spec sheet.
- **The black-dot cursor.** It never shows an arrow. A click is just the dot sitting on the target for 1–2 frames (#88, #110). It matches the minimalist brand language and reads as "attention", not "mouse".
- **The model picker is shown on a blurred background** at ~10 px (#64–67). The picked model is "Opus (Anthropic)", a product-truth detail.
- **Bold-verb input copy:** **Research** / **Analyze** / **Visualize** in bold, the object in regular. The input reads like a headline.
- **Rewrite micro-state.** The old status paragraph goes to ~20% grey before new text appears (#112), and the new bullets under "Detail" appear one line per frame (#113–114).
- **"amplify" sandwich.** Two copies of the word are cropped by the frame top and bottom, with "*your abilities*" boxed between them (#126). It's a scale shout rendered as architecture.
- **The ghost-trail fan** (#144): reference cards leave 3–4 semi-transparent copies, like a stroboscopic motion trail.
- **Ending gradient reveal.** On "reimagined" the letters are dark at the left and fade to ~10% at the right mid-reveal (#178–179). It's a soft left-to-right wipe across the word, 1–1.5 s.

## 6. Pacing curve
- **0–10 s:** very slow manifesto. Three phrases at 2–3 s each, whiteouts between them, no UI.
- **8–20 s:** the construction act (logo → icon → rail → app → phone). It's steady, a morph every ~1 s.
- **20–40 s:** feature acts at 2–3.5 s each (panes, spec input, slot reel, memory, model picker).
- **34–37 s:** a manifesto interlude ("To offer what you need when you need it"), ~4 s.
- **37–62 s:** in-app features at a dense cadence. The longest single beat is the doc edit (8.5 s).
- **62–64 s:** the "amplify" shout, the second manifesto interlude.
- **64–86 s:** notebook, collage, then the **card montage at ~1.6 s per card**, the fastest passage and the climax, converging into the cluster.
- **88–96 s:** end type at ~2 s per phrase, then the logo converge, held 1.5 s.

## 7. What makes it premium
- The UI is introduced **by being designed on screen** (keyline grid, rail, redlines). It's a "made with care" story, not a feature list.
- There's a real **light world**: photoreal architecture renders that bookend the UI and carry the manifesto lines.
- Italic serif appears *only* on the human word. The pairing does the emotional work.
- The monochrome UI lets the Office app colours become the only chroma, which reads as "your stuff".
- The cursor is abstracted into a dot, so the demo reads as intent.
- The feature montage uses a *verb + your + noun* headline embedded in each card ("Protect your time"). Parallel syntax makes six features one idea.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Light-patch typography.** Render an architectural void with a single hard sunbeam, and place the key word inside the lit patch. Fade the type in with the exposure. Change scenes by overexposing to white (0.5 s) and fading up the next room (1 s).
2. **Construct the UI from the logo.** Logo on a tile → blueprint lines → a primitive (circle) morphs into the first icon on its keyline grid → icons stack into a rail → the rail widens into a menu → pull back to the window. About one morph per 0.5–1 s.
3. **Responsive width morph.** Hold the window, then animate its width desktop → tablet → phone in 3 steps of ~0.5 s each, with the content reflowing. Keep the height and centre fixed.
4. **Redline beat.** Before interacting with a component, overlay a blue spec: a padding tint, dimension arrows and numbers, ~1.5 s. Then remove it and push in.
5. **Bold-verb slot input.** The input field is typed at headline size (~6% of frame height). Swap the whole query every ~0.5–1 s with the verb in bold.
6. **Card-headline montage.** 5–7 artifact cards, each with an embedded "Verb your noun" headline in the card's own type style. Hold 1.5–2 s each, with a 1-frame 50% ghost cross-dissolve. Finish by flying all the cards into a depth cluster (back plane blur 6–10 px).
7. **Gradient word reveal.** The roman part of a line appears hard. The italic key word wipes in left→right with a soft gradient edge ~50% of the word's width, over 1–1.5 s.
8. **Converge-to-mark ending.** The logo's primitives (four squares) start scattered on a hairline grid and slide into formation over ~1 s. The grid fades, and the mark turns from grey to black. Hold 1.5–2 s.
