# ChatGPT Work: analysis (201 frames, ≈100 s)

## 1. One-line concept
ChatGPT Work is an agentic ChatGPT for real projects. The film follows one fictional project, the "Sol" lamp line ("Project: New Lamp Concept"), from a spoken brief, through research across Gmail, Slack, Excel and Notion, a supplier spreadsheet, lifestyle images, a product page, a leadership deck and a slide edit, to async follow-up on a phone (a Slack request gets a billboard mockup). Six typed headline cards act as chapter titles, and the film opens and closes on the knot logo.

## 2. Visual world
- **Two grounds, alternating by hard cut:**
  - pure white (#FFF) for type cards and macro UI;
  - a stock-looking **starfield/nebula macOS wallpaper** under every full-window shot (#63–78, #97–103, #126–147).
  There is no texture, grid or brand ground plate; the white is a true void.
- **Type:** one bold grotesk (OpenAI Sans-like, semibold), black, centred, cap height about 4–4.5% of frame height, always 1–2 lines. It is larger than the manual's caption range, but it never shouts.
- **Accent:** a single "Work" hue that *cycles*. "Wo" enters green (#11) and settles blue (#12). "Work" enters red (#193) and settles blue (#194). A typing dot is pink (#25), then black → green → pink (#189–191). Blue (#1E88E5-ish) is the resting colour.
- **UI:** the real ChatGPT UI, very faithful and barely simplified. It's staged three ways:
  1. **Extreme macro** on white: an input corner or a model chip filling the frame (#25–31);
  2. a **cropped input bar** at 2× (#20–24, #32–47, #84–92, #104–111);
  3. a **full desktop window** at about 75% frame width, flat and square-on, with a soft shadow on the starfield (#63–78).
  There is one device: a pale-grey minimal phone (#153–170).
- **Imagery:** product photography of the fictional lamps (yellow, red, green, blue S-curve lamps), rendered as generated outputs.
- **Recurring motifs:** the grey helper-text status line (icon + grey verb phrase), the "Project / Permissions" footer tray under the input, "Worked for 2m 30s >" receipts, and numbered slide thumbnails.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Hard cut to white type card** (×6: #7, #48, #79, #148, #184, and #14 within a card). This is chapter punctuation; the only carrier is the white ground and the centred position. It's motivated by rhythm, not by cause.
- **Hard cut white macro ↔ starfield window** (#62→#63, #96→#97, #125→#126). There's no carrier; the starfield itself signals "we're in the real app now".
- **Pull-back from macro** (#31→#32: ~3× out, from button corner to full bar). This is the manual's macro → context.
- **Truck along the waveform** (#32–42). The voice recording *is* the connective line.
- **Waveform → text** (#42→#44). The object changes form (voice → transcript) in the same container.
- **Masked text wipe + icon swap** (#54–62). A status-line relay.
- **Input overlaid on the prior artifact** (#84, #104). The previous output stays visible behind the next prompt, so context carries across by layering, not by morph.
- **Blur-to-sharp generation** (#93–96, #178–183) as the "creating" verb.
- **Cut-in / cut-out on a comment pin** (#129→#130→#136). A cause-driven zoom (the pin is placed, so we go into it).
- **In-screen navigation** on the phone (#161→#162, list insertion #164).
- **Logo ↔ typed-name palindrome.** Open: logo → typed name (#6→#7). Close: typed name → backspace → logo (#196→#197).

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- **Placeholder copy swap:** "Ask anything" becomes "Do anything" in one frame (#20→#21). The product thesis is delivered through placeholder text.
- **The footer tray arrives before the user types** (#22–23). Project and permissions context are established first, and the "Ask for approval" chip is a trust cue.
- **The hue-cycling arrival state.** The newest word ("Work") or the typing dot is shown in a transient hue for one frame (green/red/pink) and settles to blue. Across the film the dot runs through black/green/pink. It's a "multicolour cursor" that stands in for a logo atom.
- **Two-weight model chip:** "5.6 Sol" in black, "Extra High" in grey (#26). The same pattern recurs small in every later input.
- **The timer ticks 0:00 → 0:01 within one frame of the stop icon appearing** (#30–31), while the camera drifts ~10%, the only hold-drift in the film.
- **Status text reveals through a soft mask:** a gradient edge ~15% of the line width (#56 "Analyzing su…"). The erase runs in the same direction, left to right, so the text "passes through" (#55, #58, #60).
- **Generated images arrive blurred:** ~30 px at 1920 on #93, and the incoming carousel card at ~15 px (#94–95). The *previous* card leaves sharp, so blur means "still being made", not depth.
- **The comment pin appears twice:** first as a toolbar affordance, then on the canvas (#128→#129). The mode switch is shown before the action.
- **An empty card before the content** (#164): the new Scheduled row appears blank for one frame, then its text fills in, a manual-style micro-state.
- **A notification pushes the device down** (#166–167) instead of overlapping it.
- **"Worked for 2m 30s"** receipts appear twice (#112, #171). The agent's time is shown as proof.

## 6. Pacing curve
- **0–10 s:** logo (3 s) → two typed cards. Calm and fully legible.
- **10–24 s:** the voice-input act. It's the most cinematic passage (extreme macro, pull-back, truck), with a UI beat every 1.5–2.5 s.
- **24–31 s:** type card → status relay at 1 app per ~1.1 s, the fastest reading passage.
- **31–40 s:** the first **8 s locked full window**. This is a breath, but a frozen one.
- **40–74 s:** a repeated loop of card → prompt typing (4 s) → result (3–6 s): images, page, deck, slide edit. The cadence is steady at ~3–4 s per beat. The densest point is #120–135 (scroll, pin, macro prompt).
- **74–91 s:** the mobile/async act. It is slower, and the phone moves gently.
- **91–100 s:** type card → dot hues → name → backspace → logo, held 2.5 s.

Rhythm is a strict **title card every 14–20 s** (#7, #48, #79, #148, #184). The film is structured like a keynote chapter list, and its climax is the ending name palindrome rather than any demo moment.

## 7. What makes it premium (vs. a normal SaaS demo)
- One project threaded through every feature. Lamp photos from act 3 reappear in the deck, the page, the billboard and even behind the next prompt (#104). You believe it's one afternoon of work.
- The extreme macro of the model chip and mic (#25–31) at ~8% frame-height type, before any full UI is shown. The first "UI" the viewer sees is a single label.
- The **status relay** replaces a spinner with four named tools, erased and re-written in place (#54–62).
- Real, specific data (82.0 readiness, $28.40 landed cost, 12,500 capacity, "Q4 marks the growth inflection point").
- Brand restraint: white, black and one hue. The logo is used only at the bookends, and the name is literally typed and un-typed.
- Trust micro-copy (Permissions: Ask for approval; Monitoring; Worked for 2m 30s) placed where the eye lands.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Hue-cycling arrival.** The newest word or cursor enters in a transient accent hue (1 frame ≈ 0.5 s, different each time) and settles to the brand accent. Across the film, cycle 3–4 hues so the colour itself reads as "multi-tool". Hard switch, no tween.
2. **Placeholder-as-thesis.** Open on the product's real input with its default placeholder, then swap one word ("Ask" → "Do") in one frame. Hold 1 s before and after.
3. **Status relay.** Fixed left-anchored slot: an icon (hard swap) + a grey verb phrase (40–50% grey). The text reveals left→right through a ~15%-wide gradient mask over ~0.5 s, holds 0.5 s, and erases left→right over ~0.5 s. Do 3–5 tools at ~1.1 s each.
4. **Voice-as-line truck.** A waveform grows leftward from a dotted baseline. The camera trucks with its head at ~40% of frame width per 2 s. At the end the waveform collapses to a spinner (0.5 s), then the transcript types into the same box.
5. **Context-under-prompt.** Each new prompt bar is overlaid on the cropped previous artifact (spreadsheet rows, product photo) so continuity is spatial layering. The prompt sits in the lower-left 60% and the artifact peeks in at the top-right.
6. **Blur-to-sharp generation.** A generated asset appears at ~30 px blur and sharpens over 2–3 frames (1–1.5 s). In carousels, blur only the incoming item.
7. **Pin → macro → pull-back edit.** Place the pin (show the toolbar state first), cut in 3× to the tinted selection with an inline composer, type the prompt, and pull back to the full window for the send click.
8. **Typed-name palindrome ending.** Colour dots (3 frames) → type the name with the accent word → backspace to a stub → hard swap to the logomark. Hold 2.5 s. Mirror the opening (logo → typed name).
