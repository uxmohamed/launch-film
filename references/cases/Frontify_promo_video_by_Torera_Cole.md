# Frontify promo (by Torera Cole), analysis (35 frames, ~17.5 s)

## 1. One-line concept
A wordless teaser for Frontify, a brand-management platform with AI guideline checking. A brand's scattered assets are read against its guidelines, reassembled into an on-brand system, filed as one physical folder, multiplied to scale, and closed on the Frontify mark. The spine is **assets → reduced to a dot → the dot "reads guidelines" → components recombine → the guideline folder → ×100 → one folder → logo**.

## 2. Visual world
- **Ground:** warm off-white or cream (#f4f0ea), flat, with no grain. It shifts slightly cooler and greyer in the "reading" and folder beats (#12–31).
- **Palette:** it's the client brand's palette, not Frontify's: olive/dark green #2f3b12, mustard/yellow #e5b300, dusty mauve #9a5a60, beige #e4cfa6, and wood tones. The yellow is the single active accent (the dot and the highlight pill).
- **Type:**
  - A heavy grotesk in the website mock ("HANDMADE FURNITURE", ~6% of frame height).
  - A regular grotesk for the reel ("Reading guideline 12", ~5% of frame height).
  - A specimen "Aa" (~15%) on the brand-board card.
  - There is no headline copy at all.
- **Staging:**
  - A flat web page mock at ~55% of frame width on the ground (#4–6), assembling and disassembling as a layout.
  - A list reel (#12–14).
  - A collage of cut-out photo and colour blocks (#16–18).
  - A brand-board card (#19–21).
  - A photographic 3D object: a kraft folder with a paper label and papers peeking out, with a real shadow (#22–31).
- **Depth:** the folder has a soft contact shadow. The collage chair has motion ghosting. Everything else is flat.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Layout reverse-build:** the same layout builds up and then un-builds to one element (#2–8). The motivation is "take a brand apart into assets".
- **Object → dot (atom):** the table icon becomes a yellow dot (#8→9).
- **Dot → bullet:** the travelling dot lands as the active-row bullet in the reel (#11→12). This is a true atom carry.
- **Swatch collapse:** the reel resolves into a single colour swatch (#14→15), then the collage assembles from it.
- **Collage snap → card** (#18→19).
- **Card → folder:** a hard cut, with continuity from the centre and scale.
- **Random-cell multiply / demultiply** (#26–29) in ~2 s.
- **Object → logo:** the folder slides out and the mark appears in the same centre.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- The guideline numbers in the reel are **out of order** (12, then 8, then 9). The reel shows *checking*, not scrolling.
- The yellow highlight pill has a soft underglow, and the next row's pill starts faint and yellow (#14): the arrival state of the reel.
- The chair in the collage leaves a 2–3-copy **ghost trail** (#18). It's stylised motion blur, not a camera blur.
- The folder shrinks during its hold (#22→25). The hold drift here is *negative*, a pull-away that anticipates the multiplication.
- Grid cells appear and disappear in a scattered order, not a raster scan. That reads as "many teams, independently".
- The film has no copy at all. The only word-like content is UI text ("Reading guideline").

## 6. Pacing curve
- 0.5 s of silence.
- A 4 s build and un-build.
- A 1.5 s dot beat: the hinge.
- 1.5 s of reel, the fastest section.
- A 3.5 s assemble.
- 2 s for the folder.
- 2 s for the multiply: the peak, the most elements on screen.
- 1 s for a single folder.
- A 2 s logo hold.

Every beat is 1.5–2.5 s: a teaser cadence with no dialogue breaks.

## 7. What makes it premium
- It explains an abstract AI feature ("checks your assets against guidelines") with no UI screenshots and no words. It uses a dot, a list reel, and a physical folder.
- It uses the *customer's* brand world (a furniture shop), so the product's value is shown as their brand looking right.
- The palette discipline holds: one accent, yellow, which is the atom.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Reverse-build to an atom.** Build a layout in 3 steps (0.5 s each), un-build it in 2, and leave one object that morphs into a 2% dot. That dot is the atom for the rest of the film.
2. **Non-sequential reel.** A slot reel whose values jump (12 → 8 → 9) signals analysis rather than browsing. Use 0.5 s per step, neighbours at 25%, and a pill highlight on the active value.
3. **Collage snap.** Swatches and a cut-out fly in diagonally (0.8 s, with a ghost trail on the hero object), then snap into a tidy card.
4. **Physical metaphor object.** A photographed real-world object (folder, envelope) stands for the digital artefact. Hold it with a slow −15% shrink.
5. **Random-cell multiply.** Pop the object into an N×M grid in a shuffled order over ~1 s, hold 0.5 s, and pop out in a shuffled order leaving one. It shows scale.
