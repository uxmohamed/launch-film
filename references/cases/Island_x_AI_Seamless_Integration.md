# Island x AI — "Seamless Integration" / AI Protect teaser (61 frames ≈ 30 s)

## 1. One-line concept
Island (the enterprise browser) launches **AI Protect**: employees can use any AI tool while IT controls where sensitive data goes. Spine: the entire script is **typed into other companies' AI chat boxes** as if the viewer (a CISO) is prompting them: "Hey AI. Show me how to make AI safe for work." → "So my people access any AI they need" → "While I control where my sensitive data goes" → "All / from / one / place" → AI "Thinking…" answers "Heard of *Island*?" → "AI Protect. By Island. The *Enterprise* Environment." → "AI that works. *For work.*" → Island logo. The prompt box is the followed object; the AIs are the set; Island is the punchline the AI itself delivers.

## 2. Visual world
- **Grounds (3 acts):** (a) deep forest green #0F3B2E with a subtle vertical gradient (#1–11) — Island brand green; (b) warm light-grey macOS-style desktop, beige-to-white gradient, with a Dock and Finder folder icons (#12–35); (c) a green radial bloom (#37–41) that becomes a lush photographic (likely AI-generated / 3D) landscape of windswept grass hills, purple wildflowers, blue sky — first as a framed card (#41), then full-bleed, then defocused ~40–60 px for the ending (#53–61).
- **Type:** everything is UI text — a neutral grotesk at ~2.5–3% of frame height inside inputs; the ending headline in white grotesk ~3% with *italic serif* for the emphasised word ("*Island*?", "*Enterprise*", "*For work.*"). No large type anywhere — the biggest text is ~3.5%.
- **UI staging:** chat inputs rebuilt in the look of several real AI products without logos: a pill input with "+" and mic/send (#1–11, ChatGPT-like), a Claude-like asterisk app in the Dock with a floating input sheet over the desktop (#12–19), a Perplexity-like input with "Pro / Research / Labs" tabs (#20–29), a stack of 4 different inputs + a terminal "> place" (#30–34), a "Thinking…" status card (#35–40). Then the Island browser window as a **frosted-glass window** over the landscape (#42–52), with a row of app favicon tiles.
- **Motifs:** the text caret; a green selection highlight that sweeps words (#4–6); the "data" file icon (a macOS document icon labelled "data") that is dragged by the cursor (#20–29).

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Push into the input (#3→#4)** — wide → macro 2.8× in ~0.5 s, the input clips off frame right; the sentence then pulls the camera left as it grows.
- **Hard cut between AI products (#11→#12, #19→#20, #29→#30)** — continuity by *the typed sentence*: each clause continues grammatically in a different vendor's input. The ground stays consistent within act 2.
- **Drag-a-file-into-the-sentence (#20–24)** — a desktop file icon labelled "data" becomes a word in the prompt: object → word.
- **Alert flash → redaction (#26–28)** — the product's verb (DLP / masking) *is* the transition within the shot.
- **One word per UI stack (#30–34)** — the sentence "All from one place" is spread across four overlapping inputs; the layout is the meaning ("one place" contains all the AIs).
- **Green bloom from behind the answer (#36–40)** — a radial glow in Island green grows from the "Thinking…" card until it becomes the new ground: a manual "dot grows into ground" variant where the source is a *glow*, not a shape.
- **Card → world (#41–42)** — the card's image content becomes the full-bleed landscape (scale-out reveal).
- **Frosted UI → defocus melt (#51–53)** — the browser window and the landscape blur together, leaving a soft ground for the tagline and logo.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- **Highlight-trail arrival:** each typed phrase is highlighted pale green as it lands and fades back to plain text over ~1–1.5 s (#4–6). It reads like the AI "reading" your input, and it is Island green — brand colour enters before the brand does.
- In the frosted browser headline, the newest 2–3 characters are green ("AI Prot" with "ot" green, #43) — same rule, restated in the final act.
- The caret keeps blinking through the 2.5 s hold (#7–11); the pill drifts slowly left during the hold so the frame never freezes.
- The Dock's active-app dot is under the asterisk app (#12) — a detail proving we're "in" that AI.
- The dragged "data" icon is a real macOS document glyph with the word typeset on it; after dropping it sits inline as a word, and the next word "goes" flows after it (#25).
- Redaction goes letter by letter from the left: "sensitive" → "xxxsitive" (#27) → "xxxxxxx" (#28): a mask-sweep, not a swap. The red flash lasts 1 frame (#26).
- "place" is typed into a *terminal* ("> place"), i.e., even CLI AI tools are covered — a joke only a technical audience reads.
- The Thinking card's progress bar advances between #35 and #38; the AI answer uses italic serif "*Island*" — the first appearance of the brand name is the answer to the opening question.
- The landscape grass is moving (wind), visible through the frosted window — ambient life through glass.
- Favicons in the browser fill in two rows, roughly 3–4 per frame (#47–49).
- Italic serif is reserved for exactly three words: "Island", "Enterprise", "For work." — the brand's voice.

## 6. Pacing curve
- 0–5.5 s: slow typing on green, a long 2.5 s hold after the question — suspense.
- 5.5–17 s: three AI products at ~4–5 s each; each has one gag (Dock app, file drag, redaction).
- 17–19.5 s: fastest beat — four words, four UIs, 2.5 s.
- 19.5–21 s: breath — "Thinking…" then the green bloom (the climax is a colour flood, not a camera move).
- 21–26 s: world reveal + product name typed on frosted glass.
- 26–30 s: melt, tagline, logo hold ~2 s. Very short ending; teaser register.

## 7. What makes it premium
- The script and the demo are the same thing: every line of copy is a prompt, so the "type on ground" and "UI demo" shot types are fused.
- It shows competitor-like AI UIs without logos, and lets *them* name Island — a narrative inversion.
- The product's actual verb (masking sensitive data) happens on a word in the sentence.
- Island is presented as an *environment* (a landscape you're inside, seen through a frosted window) — the brand metaphor ("island") made literal, but only after 20 s of neutral UI.
- Colour discipline: green appears only as highlight → glow → world.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Script-as-prompts across products.** Split the sentence into clauses; type each clause into a different chat UI (rebuilt, unbranded). Hard cut between UIs on clause boundaries, ~4–5 s per clause, one gag per UI.
2. **Highlight-trail typing.** Each newly typed phrase gets a brand-colour highlight (~30% opacity) that fades to nothing over 1–1.5 s; in later acts the newest 2–3 characters are brand-coloured instead.
3. **Object-as-word drag.** The cursor drags a file icon labelled with the noun into the sentence; the icon occupies the word's slot and the sentence continues after it.
4. **Verb on the word: redact.** Flash the input red for 1 frame, then mask the sensitive word left-to-right with x's over ~1 s (3–4 chars per frame).
5. **One word per UI stack.** A 4-word phrase spread over 4 overlapping, differently styled input fragments with 3–5% parallax.
6. **Glow-to-ground bloom.** A radial brand-colour glow grows from behind a small card over ~2 s until it's the new ground; the card's content then swaps to an image that scales out into the full-bleed world.
7. **Frosted window over live world.** Product window as backdrop-blurred glass (1 px light stroke) over moving footage; end by defocusing both (~40–60 px) and setting a small tagline + logo on the blur.
