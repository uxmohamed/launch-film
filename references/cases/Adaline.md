# Adaline — "Agent Self-Improvement Layer" launch (189 frames ≈ 94 s)

## 1. One-line concept
Adaline is an observability/evals platform for AI agents; the spine is a spoken-word manifesto ("observability is dead" → "the bottleneck is no longer the agent, it's you" → "Adaline runs the loop" → "ship agents that self-improve") told almost entirely through kinetic type riding on abstract diagram-motifs (traces, orbits, nodes), bracketed by two short founder-to-camera segments.

## 2. Visual world
- **Two-world open:** 0–9 s is a dark IDE-style JSON log (black bg, cyan/white mono), then a live-action photo of the same JSON on a monitor in a pale room. After #19 the film lives in a single world: off-white/cream (#F7F8F0-ish) with a faint pale-green tint, occasional flood of deep sage green (#556B4E-ish) as an "inverse" chapter colour.
- **Palette:** cream, sage/olive green (2–3 tints: pale lime #DCE6B8, mid sage, dark forest for pills), dark grey type. Nothing else. No gradients except soft radial shading on spheres.
- **Type:** one humanist grotesk (Söhne/Suisse-like, regular weight) for everything; body-size captions only ~2.5–3.5% of frame height (tiny, centred) — the film whispers. Two exceptions blown up for emphasis: "Every Prompt/tool call/retry/correction" wall (~5%) and "to look for" (#129, ~20% frame height, blurred). Mono only in the opening log and in micro-UI chips.
- **Recurring motif: the dotted "trace line".** A thin 1px dotted horizontal/vertical line with small tick-dots runs through nearly every caption; captions sit in a hairline dotted-border box, like a trace span node (#35–#38, #73–#79, #102–#112, #127–#128). Text literally rides a trace.
- **Other motifs:** 3D-shaded spheres with dashed orbital ellipses (#40–#53), radial node constellations (#54–#60, #101–#112), stacked green "span" pills (#30–#34), hairline grid lines (#142–#148, #183–#189) like graph-paper.
- **UI staging:** real product UI appears only twice and briefly: a trace-detail panel as a flat floating white card with soft shadow (#129–#134), and fanned "pytest.run / jest.run" log rows tilted ~-8° in perspective (#113–#117). UI is always subordinate to the sentence.
- **Live action:** founder in black hoodie, shoji-screen room, warm daylight, medium shot, locked camera.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Pull-back reveal (macro→context)** #5–#12: code macro becomes monitor becomes room. Motivates "this is a real person drowning in logs".
- **Light-projection bleed** #13–#18: screen text spills onto wall like projector light, then overexposes to white (#19). Bridges dark world → light world without a cut.
- **Line-rides-into-object** #38→#39, #72→#73: the dotted trace line extends and the camera follows it to the next subject. The line is the connective tissue for the whole film.
- **2.5D→2D flatten** #31→#32: isometric pills collapse into a flat tree.
- **Scale-to-fill circle wipe** #87→#89 and #149→#151: a circle (sphere/node) grows until it becomes the background colour; used twice, each time entering the green chapter.
- **Whip pan along line** #78→#79.
- **Desaturate dissolve** #90→#91: green frame fades to warm grey then to cream.
- **Text-in-box swap**: box persists, contents cross-fade (logo→tagline; "semantic representation"→"synthetic data"; adversarial→rare→edge).
- **White-wash over live action** #66, #71, #169, #182: video appears/disappears under a 60–80% cream overlay rather than a cut — the interview lives "inside" the paper.
- **Big-type blur → card** #129–#130: last words blown up and blurred as a backdrop, UI card rises over them.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- Opening "spotlight line": only the key line is at 100%, all other code ~15% — a whole sentence hidden in data.
- The type wall has real depth-of-field: foreground words (e.g. "Every tool" in #25) are soft by ~6–8px, midground sharp.
- Every caption box has a small dot terminal on its right edge (#73, #75, #102) like a span endpoint.
- Words reveal by **opacity from ~30% grey to full**, not by motion: e.g. #63 "is no longer" with "the" still ghosted; #107 "synthetic data" with "data" at 40%. Late words lag ~0.5 s.
- Orbiting satellite dots actually travel along dashed ellipses between frames (#46→#48).
- Ring circles "twinkle" — random individual circles switch grey→lime each frame (#102–#112), cheap life.
- Odometer: focused number full-contrast, neighbours ~35%, prefix "E" static left while digits roll.
- Founder shots are colour-graded warm but faded into the cream world via white overlay, never a hard cut.
- #45: whole composition dims to 40% before exit — a "breath out" before a new chapter.

## 6. Pacing curve
Cold open is fast (camera never stops for 9 s), then a 5 s type-wall crescendo, then a long, even, meditative middle (captions every ~2–3 s, each shot 3–6 s) that deliberately feels like reading a poem. Two human "anchors" (#66–#70, #169–#181) reset attention. Rhythm tightens in the green chapter (#149–#168: "Every day. Non-stop. Traces. Behaviours. Become. Data & Evals." ~1 word-group per second), climaxing on "Adaline runs the loop". Ending is quiet: founder, then two captions and URL on a grid, no big logo slam.

## 7. What makes it premium
- A single visual grammar (dotted trace line + boxed caption) threads 90 s of disparate content.
- Colour discipline: 2 hues total; the green flood is reserved for two moments so it lands as a chapter change.
- Type is tiny and centred — confidence; content carried by timing, not size. The one blow-up ("to look for") is therefore huge in effect.
- Diagrams are metaphors (orbits = drift, radial field = system complexity), not feature illustrations; product UI appears <8 s total.
- Live action is integrated with washes, not intercut.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Spotlight line in data** — full screen of code at 12–15% opacity, one line at 100%, 2 s hold, then lift all to 100% and dolly out 1.0→0.5 over 3 s (ease-in-out).
2. **Trace-line caption rail** — 1px dotted line across frame, caption in hairline dotted box with dot terminals; camera trucks along line 300–500 px/s to reach next caption. Swap text inside the box with 8-frame crossfade.
3. **Ghost-to-ink word reveal** — each word enters at 30% grey and rises to 100% over ~10 frames; next word starts when previous is ~70%. No translation.
4. **Repeated-word 3D wall** — stack a phrase 6–8× in a column, 3–4 columns on planes yawed 25–35°, camera trucks & rotates; DOF blur on nearest column.
5. **Object becomes background** — circle/node scales from ~0.3 to 1.6 frame width in 6–10 frames (strong ease-in), fill colour becomes new chapter bg; text inside cross-fades mid-scale.
6. **Slot-word swap** — fixed "The ___ cases"; only the middle word changes every ~1 s, and each swap adds one more branch pill to the diagram (accumulation).
7. **Odometer counter** — vertical list E01–E20 rolls up with the focus row at full contrast; 2.5 s roll, ease-out to stop, then replaced by the explanatory phrase.
8. **Cream wash in/out of live action** — 70% cream overlay dissolves off over 0.5 s to reveal talking head; reverse to exit.
