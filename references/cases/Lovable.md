# Lovable — "User connectors" feature launch (51 sheet frames; source indices run to 064 ≈ 32 s)

Note: this sheet is de-duplicated — labels show `#n (source)`, and source indices skip (e.g. #13 = 014, #19 = 022, #51 = 064). Durations below use the source index (≈0.5 s per source frame).

## 1. One-line concept
Lovable (AI app builder) launches per-user connectors: apps you build can let *their* users authenticate their own HubSpot/Drive/CRM accounts. Spine is one sentence built across the film: "Your app — their data. You build with connectors. [demo] They authenticate. Your app, their data/CRM/docs/projects. Lovable."

## 2. Visual world
- **Background:** black with a big, soft, moving aurora gradient — Lovable brand blue (#4A6CFF) to magenta/pink (#FF3FA0) to orange at the extremes. The gradient blob drifts between shots (bottom-right corner in #11, top-left in #4, full-frame pink wash in #2–#3 and #28–#36). It's the only "set".
- **Type:** heavy geometric grotesk (Lovable brand, ~Bold/Black), white, huge: "You" and "They" ~28–30% of frame height; standard lines ~10%. Tight tracking. One keyword per line gets a gradient fill (orange→pink "data" #4, #47).
- **Imagery:** stock-style but warm portrait photos of diverse users in circular crops with white rim (#6–#7, #38) — the "their" of the story. 3D-glossy app-icon tiles (Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Drive, Notion, Snowflake…) with dark rounded-square backplates and soft inner glow.
- **UI staging:** the Lovable editor and generated dashboards as floating cards with ~24px radius, slight 3D perspective tilt (~5–8° rotateY/rotateX), large soft shadow; a chat composer cropped macro; stacked sign-in cards fanned in 3D.
- **Motif:** the magenta/orange curved connector line between two icons (#24–#27) — a literal "connector" drawn as a glowing cable.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Hard cuts on type beats** — the dominant mechanism (#3→#4, #5→#6, #7→#8, #36→#37). The aurora bg shifts position on each cut, so cuts feel like camera moves in one lit room.
- **Scale-down settle** #8→#9, #37→#38: a giant word lands at ~160% then snaps to 100%, the next word joins.
- **Type → icon orbit** #12→#13: the next word appears with its visual (connectors) orbiting it.
- **Prompt → pill → icons** #21→#22→#24: the sent message becomes a "Connecting…" pill, which becomes the two connected icons — object carries the idea across three shots.
- **Horizontal motion-blur exit** #41: "authenticate" smears ~40px sideways into the result shot (whip).
- **Slot-reel** #47–#50: the noun rolls vertically with blur.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- Opening "Your app" is first a dark, embossed silhouette almost invisible on black (#1), then light (gradient) passes *through* the letters — a lighting reveal, not an opacity fade.
- Depth-of-field on the icon orbit: back-arc icons blurred ~4–6px and ~60% size of front-arc icons.
- The "Admin" label is a Figma-style multiplayer cursor tag — positions the builder as a collaborator.
- In the sign-in loop, each user's avatar replaces the pointer (#31–#36): the cursor *is* the person.
- Button colour and dashboard accent colour change per user (orange = Liam, purple = Maya, blue = Eva) — colour-coding identity across two shots.
- Gradient-filled keyword ("data") recurs identically at start and end — bookend.
- Slot-reel neighbours: previous word above & next below at ~25% opacity with vertical blur (#48 "calendar" below "CRM").

## 6. Pacing curve
Very punchy: nearly every beat ≤1 s for the first 8 s (single words), slowing slightly for the demo (1–2 s per step, 4 s sign-in loop is the longest shot), then snapping back to 1 s word beats for the recap and ending on the logo. ~32 s total, with the logo held only ~1 s — energetic, social-media cut.

## 7. What makes it premium
- The idea is written as one sentence distributed over the film; demo shots are inserted *between* words, so the film reads as a single line of copy.
- Consistent lighting world (aurora on black) makes 20+ hard cuts feel continuous.
- People are represented by avatars acting as cursors — the feature (per-user auth) is shown, not told.
- Per-user colour coding makes a data-scoping feature visually legible in 2 s.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Light-through-letters open** — start with a dark bevel/emboss of the title on black, then sweep a gradient light (0.5 s) that fills the glyphs and floods the bg.
2. **Oversize-settle word** — hero pronoun enters at 150–160% scale and eases to 100% in 4–6 frames (strong ease-out), then companion words attach.
3. **3D icon orbit around a keyword** — 16–24 icons on an ellipse tilted ~60° from camera, rotating ~15°/s, DOF blur on back arc.
4. **Message → status pill → connection** — sent prompt collapses to a gradient "Connecting…" pill (shimmer), which splits into two icons joined by a drawn cable.
5. **Avatar-as-cursor identity loop** — same UI card, repeat the action 3× with different avatar cursor, name, and accent colour; ~1.2 s per cycle.
6. **Moving aurora stage** — one blurred multi-stop gradient blob repositioned per cut (corner ↔ full wash) to give hard cuts continuity.
7. **Blurred slot-reel noun** — "their ___" with the noun rolling vertically every ~1 s; neighbours at 25% and motion-blurred.
