# Aurel (AI financial-education app) — 51 frames ≈ 25.5 s

## 1. One-line concept
A teaser for an AI finance tutor. The sentence is "What if mastering finance / felt as simple / as riding a bike? / Meet Aurel. / Learn · Simulate · Invest." One question, "Explain ETFs simply", is then followed from the input to the answer, and the film ends on "Start learning today → ✦ Aurel".

## 2. Visual world
- **Ground:** pure white. The second ground is **Impressionist paintings** (Monet: the Japanese bridge, Rouen cathedral, water lilies, haystacks, mountains), used as card art and then as a full-frame backdrop behind the app window (#27–32). The painting *is* the brand texture. Classic art is a metaphor for "timeless literacy".
- **Type:** a warm book serif (Newsreader- or Source Serif-like), regular weight, at about 2.2% of frame height for captions. It is set tiny and centred with huge emptiness. The UI uses a small grotesk plus serif headlines ("Build your financial knowledge").
- **Palette:** black, white, and the paintings' pastel greens and blues. There is no brand accent colour; the black send button is the only solid mass.
- **Staging:**
  - a frameless app window on the painting, inset about 2% from the frame edges (almost full-bleed);
  - a macro sidebar crop;
  - an extreme macro of the input text;
  - a Dock-like glass tray of real macOS icons with a soft shadow.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Blur-scatter word exit** (#3, #6, #14, #26, #45): the words blur about 6–10 px, drift up 1–2% and rotate ±2–3° independently, staggered left to right by about a frame. It is the film's signature exit, and the reverse is used for entrances.
- **Inline icon → swarm** (#7→8): the inline emoji icon spawns copies around the line. The emphasis is the metaphor itself.
- **Tray contraction** (#10→12): a Dock of generic apps shrinks to the one that matters, so "Meet Aurel" reads as the survivor.
- **Slot reel over a moving carousel** (#15–23): the text reel is vertical and the image carousel horizontal, in two perpendicular motions.
- **Painting → backdrop** (#26→27): the card art becomes the ground plate for the full UI.
- **Macro type → pull-back** (#34→35): about a 20× scale change in one cut, from giant caret text to the full input card.
- **Push to affordance** (#37→38): the send button alone, as the cause of the answer.
- **Ghost-in answer** (#39→41): the result fades up rather than streaming.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- The bike icon arrives **before** its word (#7). The word "bike" types in after it, so the icon is read first.
- The swarm bikes are the same line icon in slightly varied sizes (±20%), at roughly even angular spacing, not random.
- Before contracting, the Dock tray slides left by about 40% of its width (#10→11), then loses its outer icons.
- The next slot-reel word previews at about 25% grey **directly below** (#17 "Simulate", #20 "Invest"), in line with the manual's slot reel.
- The app window never sits on white. Its ground is always a painting, so the UI inherits the art.
- The caret in the macro (#33–34) is about 1/8 of the glyph width: thick, drawn, not the system hairline.
- The UI spelling "Ressources" is left in (real UI, not rebuilt copy).

## 6. Pacing curve
- The first 12.5 s are type only, at 1–1.5 s per phrase, with the icon swarm as the first flourish.
- The middle (#15–26) is a steady reel.
- The product act is compressed into about 8 s: wide → macro → extreme macro → send → answer, a 1 s-per-state ladder.
- It exhales with "Start learning today" and ends on 1.5 s of logo plus 1 s of white. There is no scale shout except the macro input.

## 7. What makes it premium
- The art-history ground gives a finance product warmth that no gradient can.
- The serif, the tiny type and the extreme white space make it feel editorial, not fintech.
- The one-question follow-through (Explain ETFs → answer) is complete in 5 s.
- The exits are more crafted than the entrances (blur-scatter with rotation).

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Blur-scatter exit.** Per word: blur 0→10 px, translate up 1–2%, rotate a random ±3°, opacity 1→0 over about 0.4 s, staggered about 0.05–0.1 s left to right. Entrances use the reverse with a smaller rotation.
2. **Inline icon swarm.** An icon at cap height inside the sentence spawns 12–16 copies in a loose ring at 1.5–3× the line width. Hold 1 s, then cut.
3. **Survivor tray.** Show a familiar app dock, slide it, then remove the outer icons symmetrically until only your icon is left. The next card names it.
4. **Painting ground plate.** Use one artwork style (a public-domain series) for the card art, and reuse it full-bleed behind the window.
5. **Perpendicular reel.** A vertical word slot reel (0.5 s per roll) above a horizontal image carousel scrolling constantly.
6. **Caret-macro sandwich.** Wide card → 20× macro of the text being typed (two frames) → back to the card → the send button alone → the result.
