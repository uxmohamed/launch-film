# Apple — "Apple has a plan" (Carbon neutral 2030) — 217 frames; content #1–194 ≈ 97 s, then 11.5 s of black

## 1. One-line concept
A pure kinetic-typography film for Apple's 2030 carbon-neutral pledge. It is one long spoken sentence set in SF Pro, and a hand-drawn green "editor" layer keeps annotating, correcting and illustrating it. The spine is "Apple has a plan" → proof clauses → "Apple has a plan", a perfect bookend.

## 2. Visual world
- **Three grounds, hard-cut:**
  - light grey (#EEEEEE);
  - Apple-environment green (about #00D95A);
  - black.

  The ground flips every 1–6 s, usually on a clause boundary. Type is black on grey, black on green, and white on black.
- **Type:** SF Pro Display Medium, centred, cap height about 4.5–5% of frame height. It is one size for about 90% of the film.
- **Second voice:** a hand-lettered green all-caps marker face ("AND A PROMISE.", "ALREADY", "STILL", "HOW", "YOU", "EVERYTHING", "2030"). It appears with proofreader's carets (^), hand-drawn underlines, emphasis rays, arrows, parentheses and doodle icons.
- **Imagery:**
  - flat hand-drawn green doodles: trees, stacked boxes, a factory, smoke, a globe, arrows, an iPhone/iPad/Watch outline, and a plug;
  - four brief moments of grey studio footage of recycling machinery (the Daisy robot, shredded aluminium, tin, a tungsten gripper, a falling plastic frame) with a white word overlaid (#54–59, #109–111, #133–135).
- There is no depth, no shadow and no camera move. Everything is flat and orthographic.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Hard ground cut on a clause:** the dominant mechanism, about 30 times. The motivation is the spoken beat: grey means a statement, green the pledge, black a contradiction or aside ("wait", "no", "But").
- **In-place letter replacement:** #9–10 "has a plan" → "To make … carbon". The logo glyph is the fixed anchor and the surrounding letters swap.
- **Letter → seedling → forest → ground:** #63→69. The verb "recycle/grow" is shown as type literally sprouting, the trees fill the frame, and a cut to green follows. This is a verb transition.
- **Page-curl:** #71. The green sheet peels off to reveal grey. It is motivated by "paper".
- **Ink-blob flood:** #83→85. The factory's smoke blob expands to become the next ground.
- **Arrow in from off-frame:** #97–108. Each noun is delivered by arrows, standing for supply-chain flow.
- **Footage with a word over it:** #54–59. A hard-cut slideshow at 0.5 s.
- **Tracking-out → logo:** #191→192. The URL's characters spread and then vanish, leaving the logo.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- The **proofreader layer** is consistent. Every addition enters with a caret (^) at the insertion point (#6, #45, #124, #175), as if the brand is editing its own claim live. The hand-lettering is written stroke by stroke over 2–3 frames (#6 "AND A" → #7 "AND A PROMISF" → #8 "PROMISE."). Mid-write frames show a misspelled partial letter.
- The **checkbox** (#17–19) appears empty and is ticked in green only after "done that" completes.
- **Doodle icons have a hand-lettered label and a tiny arrow** pointing to the icon (#37–39): a label, a pointer and an object, all hand-drawn.
- **Arrow typology encodes meaning:** smooth arcs for manufacturers, loops for testers and assemblers, rigid orthogonal elbows for "dis-assemblers" (#104).
- **Baseline scatter** at #91: "it's also how they're made" lands with each word at a different baseline (±3% h), then snaps flat. It is a playful "made" wobble.
- The **Daisy robot arm** appears to physically pick up the word "upgrading" (#109). Footage interacts with type.
- **2022 vs 2030** (#148–149): only the year swaps, in hand-lettering. It is a single-slot reel.
- **Neon glow** is used once, on "100% renewable." (#158–162). Energy is the only glowing thing.
- The **ending URL tracks out** (#191 "apple . com / 2030") before collapsing. It is a subtractive letter-spacing exit.

## 6. Pacing curve
- The cadence is almost metronomic: about 1 word per 0.5 s for 97 s. Variety comes entirely from the ground flips and from the doodle layer.
- The fastest passages are the material slideshow (#54–59, 0.5 s per cut), the doodle device slot (#37–39) and the label burst (#155).
- The breaths are "(This is a big one)", a whisper on green held 1.5 s, and "EVERYTHING is going carbon neutral", about 5 s, the longest hold.
- The climax is "all … of YOU?" (#137, the only type scale shout), closely followed by the glowing "100% renewable".
- The ending is subtractive: sentence → URL → logo → black, with an exact bookend of "has a plan."

## 7. What makes it premium
- It is a 97-second film with zero product shots, carried by copy and two type voices.
- The hand layer isn't decoration. It is the brand annotating and upgrading its own promise, and it enacts "a plan and a promise".
- Ground colour does semantic work (statement, pledge, contradiction).
- Only one type size for 90% of the runtime, so "all" and the whisper both land.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Proofreader insertion.** A caret (^) appears at the insertion point and an accent hand-lettered word is written above it along a 5–10° upward slant over 2–3 frames. The main line doesn't move.
2. **Semantic ground flip.** Use three grounds mapped to meaning. Hard-cut on the clause boundary; never cross-fade.
3. **Letter-to-object sprout.** Chosen letters shrink to dots in 1 frame and are replaced by a sprouting icon of the same height. The object then multiplies until it becomes the next ground (about 2 s total).
4. **Arrow delivery.** Each list noun arrives with 3–5 arrows from the frame edges converging on it. Change the arrow style per noun. Hold 1 s per noun.
5. **Whisper for the biggest claim.** Drop to about a third of body size for a parenthetical aside, alone on the pledge colour, for 1.5 s.
6. **Doodle slot.** A fixed word plus a hand-drawn icon with a label and pointer that swaps every 0.5 s (iPhone → iPad → Watch).
7. **Tracking-out logo exit.** Widen the URL letter-spacing by about 30% over 0.5 s, then cut to the logo alone at the same centre.
