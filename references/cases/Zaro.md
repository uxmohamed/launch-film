# Zaro — analysis (138 frames, ~69 s)

## 1. One-line concept
Zaro is a prompt-to-app builder: apps plus agents plus connected files, answering from your context. The spine is a chain of **two-line couplets**, each with a typed second line:
- "Build an app. / With a sentence."
- "Add in agents. / They keep it running."
- "Simple to start. / Powerful to scale."
- "Connect your mess / files / tools."
- "Chaos becomes context"
- "Ask anything. / Your files answer."
- "Works where you work."
- "You didn't just build an app. / You grew a system."

One deal-tracker app and one FluxCo question are followed through the product.

## 2. Visual world
- **Two grounds, alternated as inhale/exhale:**
  - dark warm brown (#231a18 in the open, #4f4541 taupe-brown for the couplets, #1e1614 at the finale) for type;
  - warm greige paper (#ebe8e3) for UI.

  A lavender blurred gradient (#11–13) is used once, for "Meet".
- **Accent:** lavender (#b7a8f5). It is used for:
  - the caret;
  - the newest word ("and agents", "one", "system.");
  - the agent cursors;
  - chart bars;
  - the CTA pill.

  Agent identities get small secondary flags (orange, yellow, green, cyan).
- **Type:** a neutral grotesk, medium weight, ~5% of frame height for couplets. Line 1 is white; line 2 is grey (~55%) while being typed. The mono appears only in the CTA "TRY FOR FREE AT ZARO.AI". **Dot-matrix/pixel type** is used for "Chaos becomes context" (#76–78).
- **Texture:** a **dot-matrix pixel field**: faint grey squares clustered at the frame edges (#7–10, #134–138), pixelated agent cursors (#41–45), and a dot grid that morphs into a card (#79).
- **UI staging:**
  - full app window at ~70% of frame width on greige, used as an orientation wide (#28–32, #101–103);
  - many **macro crops** where the frame cuts the UI (chat panel #33–40, pipeline cards #52–54, folder grid #82–85, file list #86);
  - a **giant input** where the text is ~8% of frame height (#94–95);
  - split-screen with type on the left third on brown and UI on the right on greige (#58–66, #107–114).

  Shadows are soft and very light. There's a hard device (a phone) only for "Works where you work" (Slack), which is someone else's platform.
- **Motifs:** the lavender caret; orbit rings (dotted circles) around an app (#121–126); concentric rounded-rect ripples behind the agents card (#46).

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Dark couplet ↔ paper UI**: a hard cut on ground change every 4–8 s. This is the main rhythm.
- **Caret as atom**: the caret types every couplet and goes giant at "Meet" (#11). The "Connect your ___" reel is done by backspacing.
- **Pixel dissolve** (#13, #67, #76–79): a checkerboard or dot-matrix resolve, which is the "chaos → context" verb.
- **Dot-field → UI card** (#79→80): the dots of the type become the outline of the next app.
- **Status-log → headline** (#110→111): the last log item ("Read Slack") stays in place while the words around it change into the next claim. A text-lineage cut.
- **Inline object → hub** (#120→121): the app card between words becomes the centre of the orbit diagram.
- **Iris to dot → dot to logo** (#131→133). The logo also collapsed into a blob at #18, so the mark both opens and closes the film through a single dot.
- **Glow card = generating** (#27): the blank white card glows before the UI fills in.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- The chips inside "Apps [ ] and [ ] agents" are **agent cursors with labels** (an arrow + a pill), foreshadowing the pixel cursors at #41 and the Deal Monitor drag at #52.
- "one prompt" gets a **second lavender pulse** after the line completes (#8). Emphasis is re-applied after the arrival, not only on arrival.
- The status log uses real product verbs and counts ("Searching 2142 Files").
- In the giant input (#94–95), the text is so big the caret is 8% of frame height. It's a caret-follow truck at the macro extreme.
- The Deal Monitor agent cursor is the *orange* one from #41. Agent colour identity is consistent.
- Chart values tick between frames ($93K → $60K → $46K; bars resize) as the proof beat.
- A "Planning…" shimmer is the only spinner-like state (#38–40), and it's text-based.
- The pixel field in the end card sits at the bottom-left, matching the #7–10 field position (a bookend).

## 6. Pacing curve
It opens fast with type (1–2.5 s beats), then the logo at ~7 s (front-loaded). From there, a steady alternation of **couplet (2–2.5 s) → UI proof (3–6 s)**, seven times. There's a density peak in the "Connect your…" slot reel and the status-log column (#67–75, #107–110), where nothing needs reading in full. The climax is the orbit pull-back → "You grew a system." (#121–131). It ends by subtraction: the system field irises to one dot → mark → CTA, held ~2.5 s.

## 7. What makes it premium
- The couplet structure makes the whole film a poem. Every UI section is answered by a line.
- One app (Deal Tracker) and one question (FluxCo) are followed across four features.
- Agents are **characters** (coloured pixel cursors with names) rather than a list of features.
- There are two texture languages (dot-matrix and clean UI), and the transition between them *is* the value proposition ("Chaos becomes context").
- The CTA pill is the only mono type, lavender, small.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Typed-couplet interstitials.** Line 1 static and white; line 2 typed in 55% grey with an accent caret at ~5 chars/frame. Hold 1 s after the full stop, then hard cut to the UI ground.
2. **Backspace slot reel.** "Connect your mess→files→tools": delete the last word and retype it. The background props swap to match each word.
3. **Agent-as-cursor.** Give each agent a coloured cursor + name flag. Show it once in pixel art as a character (#41), then have the same colour do a real UI action (a drag) later.
4. **Status-log column.** Process steps append at the bottom of a left column at 2 lines/s. The newest line is 100% ink, older lines 40%. The last line becomes the next headline by in-place word replacement.
5. **Glow-card generation.** A blank white card with a 30–40 px soft outer glow for ~0.5 s, then the UI skeleton, then content.
6. **Dot-matrix ↔ UI morph.** Render the thesis in dot-matrix type. The dots reorganise into the outline of the next card (~0.5 s), then the real UI fades in.
7. **Orbit pull-back to a system field.** The object at the centre of dotted orbit rings. Pull back ~4× to show many orbits, blur the field ~10 px, and set the thesis over it.
8. **Dot iris ending.** Iris the scene into one black dot on the paper ground, then the dot blooms into the logomark.
