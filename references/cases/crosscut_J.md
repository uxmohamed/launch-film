# Crosscut J — Superpower, Arena, Lovable 2.0, LaunchAnything, Island x AI

Films: Superpower health launch (85 s), Arena/LMArena identity (75 s), Lovable 2.0 platform launch (80 s), LaunchAnything studio reel (27 s), Island AI Protect teaser (30 s).

## 1. Patterns that recur across these films

1. **Typing is the universal engine.** All 5 films type their copy with a visible caret: Arena (specimens and logo), Island (every line is a chat prompt), Lovable (prompts, "cod#|", "/dev mo}"), LaunchAnything (URL "…no" → "…now"), Superpower ("sup" → "superpower", the chat questions). The caret is an atom in 3 of them. Arena and Island end with the caret, or with typed type, next to the logo.
2. **Ground rotation = chapter coding, with 3 grounds at most.** Superpower: orange collage / black HUD / white clinical. Arena: paper / charcoal. Lovable: cream / gradient / black. LaunchAnything: black / white / photo. Island: green / light desktop / landscape. The ground changes at every chapter card. It is never used as a background to decorate a single beat.
3. **Italic serif as the voice of emphasis inside a roman line.** Arena ("The *new* frontier", "the *real world*", *Scholastic Primaries*) and Island ("Heard of *Island*?", "The *Enterprise* Environment", "*For work.*") both do this. It's a mixed-family emphasis that the manual's "one family + mono" rule doesn't cover.
4. **The brand colour arrives before the brand does.** Island's green shows up first as a typing highlight (#4), then as a glow (#36), then as the world. Superpower's orange is the "problem" world before the wordmark. Lovable's gradient spheres open the film (#1) eight seconds before the logo.
5. **Counters tick in only 3 steps, each 0.5 s.** Lovable goes 30,952 → 45,966 → 51,976. Superpower goes "over 89 → 99 → 100 markers". Arena's CI scores tick 1448 → 1462 → 1472. The deltas decelerate. That's the low end of the manual's 3–7 steps: short and legible.
6. **Slot reels run at 0.5 s per item** (Superpower conditions #109–117, Lovable domains #117–121). Arena's keyword reel runs at about 1 s. The fast reels always sit over a moving object: a drifting modal, or an image that changes per row.
7. **A press causes a zoom or a pull-back.** LaunchAnything: a cursor press on "Launch it", then a 6× push through the button. Lovable: an icon, then a 5× pull-back to the toolbar, then a click. Arena: a grid cell highlighted, then a 6–8× tunnel dive.
8. **Endings subtract to one element on an emptied ground.** Superpower drains the colour from its wordmark (orange → grey → black). Arena reduces to a lone blinking caret, then the lockup. Island defocuses the world, leaving a small logo. Lovable reduces to a heart icon on black, then the URL.
9. **The first mention of the brand is a reveal line inside the copy:** "Meet Lovable", "Heard of *Island*?", "superpower" shouted as outline letters. The brand is never simply a title card at t = 0.

## 2. Contradictions between the films (opposite choices, and why)
- **Where the product first appears.** Superpower withholds all UI for 32 s (38% of runtime) to build an emotional "problem world". Island is UI from frame 1, because the UI *is* the script. Lovable shows UI at about 20 s after a manifesto. What decides it: a consumer/emotional product delays the UI; a B2B/tooling product leads with it.
- **One shout vs. many.** Lovable has at least 6 shouts at 25–35% of frame height ("knows", "Faster", "day", "what", "built", "Now"). Island has none (max about 3.5%). Arena's shouts are the type specimens themselves, at 70–90% height. Superpower has one (the "SU" outline). Lovable's energy comes from repeated shouts, while Island's restraint suits security buyers.
- **Device frames.** LaunchAnything and Lovable put screens in photoreal rooms (a desk with a plant, dark rocks). Superpower uses a tablet once. Arena and Island are frameless. LaunchAnything's device is justified (the product is other people's films). Lovable's photographic laptop is about "a real site, live in the world".
- **Real vs. rebuilt UI.** Arena and Lovable show real product output (generated images, websites, code) at full fidelity. Island rebuilds competitor UIs without branding. Superpower ghosts its UI to about 40% contrast (#64–68) before isolating components.
- **One arrival state vs. several.** Island sticks to one rule (green highlight or green newest characters). Arena sticks to one (a semantic caret colour). Lovable mixes four: glyph scramble, ghost-grey, per-letter baseline bounce, and blur flare.

## vs. the manual

### (a) Techniques NOT in the manual (name + recipe)
1. **Two-world film (problem world → product world).** Act I is a hot, textured duotone photo collage (grain, halation, stripe slices, pixel silhouettes), cut every 0.5 s, with questions in the viewer's voice set over a soft blob. Act II is a white ground with hairline construction lines, on 1–2.5 s beats. The wordmark is the hinge. The split is about 35:65. (Superpower #1–61 vs #62–169.)
2. **Outline-to-fill wordmark hinge.** Draw the logo letters as a 1–2 px outline with visible bezier anchors at about 50% frame height. Truck across the last problem-world image (about 1 s), fill with the brand gradient (0.3 s), white out, then retype the wordmark small inside a construction cell. (Superpower #59–63; Arena's "e" with handles, #44–46.)
3. **Anxious-word dropout.** The key word of a worried question sits on the brightest part of a blurred blob, so it nearly vanishes. The blob's hue cycles per question (about 1 s). Uncertainty becomes typography. (Superpower #37, #41, #45.)
4. **Editing-verb transitions.** Every exit is a text-editor action: backspace (the caret turns red one frame before), select-all (inverse black block), or an in-place roman ↔ italic toggle. (Arena #23–43.)
5. **Semantic caret colour.** The caret is about 0.12 em wide. It is blue while typing, red just before a delete, and the accent colour in product scenes. It only blinks when idle. (Arena.)
6. **Specimen-as-copy.** Show the typeface by typing adjectives that describe the brand ("Bookishly", "Academic", "Perfectly", "Precise"), with live metadata rows (family / styles / glyph preview) that highlight the active style. (Arena #26–47.)
7. **Blueprint → render → glyph logo build.** A wireframe with dimension leaders becomes a rendered 3D object in a framed box, shrinks about 5× on dark, and swaps to a flat line glyph. The wordmark is then typed beside it. (Arena #5–17.)
8. **Script-as-prompts across products.** Each clause of the sentence is typed into a different (unbranded) AI chat UI, with a hard cut on each clause boundary (about 4–5 s per clause). The answer names the brand. (Island.)
9. **Object-as-word drag.** The cursor drags a file icon labelled "data" into the sentence, where it takes the noun's slot, and typing continues after it. (Island #20–25.)
10. **One word per UI stack.** "All / from / one / place" is spread over 4 overlapping, differently styled input fragments with 3–5% parallax. The layout means "one place". (Island #30–34.)
11. **Highlight-trail typing.** Each newly typed phrase gets a 30% brand-colour highlight that fades over 1–1.5 s. In later acts, the newest 2–3 characters are brand-coloured instead. (Island #4–6, #43.)
12. **Glow-to-ground bloom.** A soft radial glow in brand colour (not a hard-edged dot) grows from behind a small card over about 2 s until it becomes the new ground. Then the card swaps to an image and scales out into the full-bleed world. (Island #36–42.)
13. **Giant prompt pill with inverse aura.** The prompt pill is about 60% of frame width with a gradient aura. As the camera pulls back 3×, the aura thickens and saturates, so the visual weight stays constant. On send, the pill compresses to a thin bar and re-expands into the app card. (Lovable #47–55.)
14. **Icon-first pull-back.** This is the inverse of "push to affordance". Open on the feature icon at 40–50% of frame height, then pull back about 5× over 1.5 s to reveal it's a button in the toolbar, and click it on arrival. (Lovable #86–90.)
15. **Named-cursor continuity.** Collaborator cursors ("Lucy", "Jonathan") first appear as typography companions to a chapter title. The same names reappear in the Invite modal and as live cursors on the dashboard. (Lovable #69–83.)
16. **People → UI shrink.** A row of 5 face circles shrinks about 4× into the app's avatar stack next to Invite/Publish. (Lovable #76–79.)
17. **Ground → logo-shape ending.** Under the last line of copy, the full-frame gradient blob slowly reshapes into the logo silhouette (a heart) over about 3 s. Then cut to the small logo on black. (Lovable #145–152.)
18. **Data-to-marker hand-off across a ground flip.** Chart boxes drop their numbers and become empty coloured squares. The ground flips to dark, and the same squares are now legend markers. (Arena #77–79.)
19. **Output-wall collapse.** Dozens of real outputs sit on 4–5 depth layers. Pull back about 5× over 2 s, then collapse them to a single card outline that frames the next scene. (Arena #112–118.)
20. **Light-sweep type reveal.** The glyphs start dark on black. A blue → white gradient passes left to right through them in about 0.5 s. Between words the frame is almost black, so the light itself is the transition. (LaunchAnything #1–6.)
21. **Colour-temperature arrival.** A new word or URL arrives in brand blue or blue neon, then cools to neutral within about 0.5 s. (LaunchAnything #9–10, #36–38, #46–48.)
22. **Continuous set, cutting screen.** Hold one photographic device set with moving ambient light (sun shadow through leaves) for about 7 s. Cut only the screen content, every 0.5–1 s, and tighten the framing about 1.3× every 2.5 s. (LaunchAnything #16–31.)
23. **Selection-driven 3D glow.** A sidebar selection steps one row every 0.5 s, and a mint glow moves across a frosted 3D body to the matching organ. UI state is world state. (Superpower #91–96.)
24. **Inline product object, then re-orient carry-over.** A product (a blood tube) sits at cap height between words. In the next shot it rotates 90° and scales about 3× to become the backdrop for the next clause. (Superpower #77–82.)
25. **Mixed-family italic emphasis.** Set emphasised words in italic serif inside roman grotesk or serif lines, and use it for 3–5 words per film. (Arena, Island.)

### (b) Where these films CONTRADICT the manual
- **"Break the rule once per film" / one scale shout.** Lovable 2.0 shouts at least 6 times (#7, #23, #37, #110–112, #145) and still reads as premium: repeated shouts at a 0.5 s cadence become a *rhythm*, not an emphasis. Island has zero shouts.
- **"Pick one arrival state per film."** Lovable uses scramble (#8), ghost-grey (#12, #141), per-letter baseline bounce (#29) and blur flare (#14).
- **"Slot-reel swap 0.8–1 s."** Superpower (#109–117) and Lovable (#117–121) swap every 0.5 s. That works because the fixed part of the sentence was already read, and a moving object carries the eye.
- **"Dot fill to new ground: 0.2–0.45 s."** Island's glow-to-ground takes about 2 s (#36–40) and is the climax.
- **"Full window 1–3 times, 1.5–5 s; product UI 45–80% of runtime."** Lovable shows the full tilted editor at least 6 times (#55, #64–67, #91–93, #106–108, #134–138). Arena (~25%) and Superpower (~30%) sit well below 45% UI share.
- **"3D tilt on every card is cheap."** Lovable tilts every editor shot 10–20° and every modal about 5°. It holds up because the tilt is consistent and changes during pull-backs.
- **"Device only when the platform is the point."** LaunchAnything and Lovable (#44–46, #125–126) use photoreal laptops as "shipped, in the real world" proof. Superpower uses a tablet with a floating stylus (#97–100).
- **"No AI glow clichés / no stock footage."** LaunchAnything puts a neon glow on type (#46) and uses stock rocket/Earth footage as a literal pun (#42–44). This is the weakest part of that film, so it partly confirms the rule.
- **"Front-load proof: logo by 20–35%."** Superpower's wordmark lands at about 36% (#61) and its first UI at 38%. That's a consumer-emotional exception, and it works because Act I is itself high craft.
- **"Type is small (1.5–3.5%)."** Arena's specimens run at 20–90% of frame height for about 20 s straight. In a brand-identity film the type is the product.
- **"Caret blink 1 s cycle."** Arena and Island don't blink while typing, only when idle (Arena #131–132; Island #7–11).

### (c) Manual claims these films strongly confirm
- **The film is a sentence.** All 5 are, and Island makes it literal: every clause is a prompt.
- **Follow one object.** The Island prompt box; the Lovable prompt → app → published site; the Superpower body (eye → dancer → mannequin → eye).
- **Brand atom as transition engine.** The Arena caret and column; the LaunchAnything triangle (cursor → rocket → logo); the Lovable heart (dot field → logo → gradient → heart); the Superpower crosshair (HUD → grid cell → registration box).
- **Chapter coding by ground.** It holds in every film.
- **Show the verb.** Island's redaction happens on the word. Arena's edit verbs (delete, select, italicise) are the transitions.
- **End by subtraction, with a 2–5 s hold.** Superpower drains colour; Arena goes down to a lone caret; Island defocuses. Holds are 2–6 s.
- **Counters tick in decelerating steps**, but only 3 steps (see §1.5).
- **Frosted glass over footage** (Island #43–51) matches the manual's backdrop 20–30 px plus a 1 px stroke.
- **The CTA zoom-through** exists as described. In LaunchAnything it lands on a photographic scene rather than a glyph, a variant worth adding.
