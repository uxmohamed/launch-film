# Medium — "Reposts now take a note"

41 frames, about 20 s. A single-feature teaser.

## 1. One-line concept
Medium's repost button can now carry a note. The film follows **one repost** from the headline, to the article's action bar, to the menu, the note, the published note, the people who responded, and finally the logo. The spine is the sentence "Reposts now take a note → See what [David/Julie/Jack/Matt] … everyone said".

## 2. Visual world
- **Ground = physical textures that change with each clause.** There are three:
  - a sawn-log end-grain (radial cracks, orange grain under a cool blue-grey tint) for the open and the action bar (#1–15);
  - dark slate paper covered in handwritten cursive on a faint grid, for the "note" (#16–26);
  - a grey chalkboard with a single chalk swirl, for "See what" (#27–31).

  Flat light grey or white is kept for the real Medium UI crops (#6–9, #34–41).
- **Type.** The film uses a white grotesk for its own copy, with no serif anywhere. The only serif is the brand's: the huge names (#27–31) and the "Medium" wordmark (#39–41). Captions sit at about 4% of frame height. The opening "Rep⟲sts" is about 18%.
- **UI staging.** The real article UI appears as a macro crop that bleeds off the frame edges (#6–9: title at about 8% of frame height, cropped on the left). Then the action bar is **lifted off the page into a floating white rounded card** on the texture (#10), with a very soft shadow. The compose modal is a floating card at about 85% of frame width (#16).
- **Colour.** Almost none: white UI, a black "Repost" pill, and texture tones. The accent is the photography in #27–33.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Shout → sentence shrink** (#1→#2). The logotype *becomes* the first word of the caption. It's motivated because the feature name is the subject.
- **Row lift-off** (#9→#10). Page context is deleted while the component survives, and the ground switches from the UI grey to the brand texture.
- **Paper-tear reveal** (#15→#16). The next texture tears in behind the clicked menu. It's motivated by "note": the paper is the note.
- **Push-to-affordance** (#23→#24): a push onto the Repost pill before the click.
- **Photo-window slot reel** (#27–31). The window keeps its position while its content and width change, and the handles show it as an editable object.
- **Window → full bleed** (#31→#32). The strip of faces expands until it becomes the whole frame.
- **Hard cuts** everywhere else, carried by the followed object (the repost count "57" returns at #34).

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- The repost icon has its own micro-animation (#12→#13, the arrows split apart) before the menu row is chosen.
- "now" lands lower than "Reposts" at #2. It's a late tail with a vertical offset, not a blur.
- "See what" changes style from roman to italic when the handles appear (#27→#28), as if "edited".
- The big names are deliberately cropped by the bottom frame edge (only the top 70% of the letters is visible).
- The character counter proves the typing (0/280 → 71/280). The number is the proof, not the text.
- The same count, "57", is on screen in #8 and again in #34. It's continuity by data.

## 6. Pacing
- 0–2.5 s: logo shout, then the thesis line, held 1.5 s.
- 2.5–7.5 s: UI clauses at a fast 1–2 s each.
- 8–12 s: the one "reading" beat, the note typing (4 s).
- 12–16 s: the fastest part, the 0.5 s name slot and the collage.
- 16–18 s: proof (the chip, then the feed).
- 18–20 s: the wordmark, held only about 1.5 s. It's a short ending with no subtraction beat.

## 7. What makes it premium
- A physical texture world (wood, handwriting, chalk) instead of a gradient. Each texture is a metaphor for its clause, and each is swapped by a motivated tear.
- The real UI is barely restyled. It's simply lifted out of its page.
- The logotype hack (an icon as a letter) states the feature in the very first frame.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Icon-as-letter logotype.** Replace one letter of the feature name with the product icon, at about 18% of frame height. Hold 0.5 s, then scale it to 4% as the first word of the caption (0.4 s, expo-out).
2. **Lift-off onto the world.** From the macro UI, keep one component. Cut or dissolve the page to the brand ground (0.3 s) while the component turns into a white card with a 20 px shadow. Slide it about 25% toward where the next thing will open.
3. **Tear-through ground change.** On the click, a torn paper silhouette (about 110% of the menu's size) appears behind the menu with the next texture inside. Cut to the next scene one frame later.
4. **Image slot reel with handles.** Fixed caption, a fixed photo window, and a name in a huge cropped serif. Swap the photo and name every 0.5 s, and widen the window 10–20% per swap. End by expanding the window to full bleed.
5. **Counter-as-typing proof.** Show the character counter ticking alongside the typed note, and push in on the submit button only after the counter settles.
