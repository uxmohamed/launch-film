# Veryfront — analysis (61 frames, ≈30 s)

## 1. One-line concept
Veryfront is a no-code builder for business AI agents. The film is one linear build wizard, **Choose Agent → Customize Agent (instructions, integration, model, knowledge) → Your agent is ready → use it on a contract → approved**, bookended by the same logo animation. It works as a 30 s "how it works" loop: three blue chapter cards, UI cards on warm grey, and one oversized cursor driving everything.

## 2. Visual world
- **Two surfaces only:**
  1. Warm light grey (#ECEAE6-ish) for the UI.
  2. Saturated flat azure (#0AA0F0-ish) for chapter cards, overlaid with 1 px white outline ellipses (~20% opacity) arranged like orbits or a wireframe sphere (#9–11, #19–20, #37–39).
- **Type:** rounded geometric sans (Gilroy/Circular-like). Chapter titles are white at ~7% frame height, UI titles medium weight ~3%. The wordmark is dark grey, with the logomark a small **iridescent gradient sphere** (pink→orange→teal→blue).
- **UI staging:**
  - Single white cards (radius ~16 px, soft ~30 px shadow at ~6% opacity) centred on grey. A "wizard modal" with a title at top-left and a black "Next" pill at top-right.
  - Agent tiles float at multiple depths with **depth-of-field blur** (#12–18).
  - The dark chat bubble is near-black (#1E1E1E) against white response cards.
- **Cursor:** a huge azure arrow-cursor (~8–10% of frame height, 3D-ish with a lighter bevel and drop shadow). It is the brand's protagonist and is in almost every UI shot.
- **Motif:** the blue colour itself. The cursor, the chapter cards, the Compliance Agent icon and the "Next" button hover fill are all the same azure. The gradient sphere logomark returns at start and end.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Sphere smear-in** (#1): the logomark enters with a horizontal motion streak. A single-frame motion-blur trail, not a blur filter.
- **Mask reveal from behind the logomark** (#2→4) and its **reverse** (#5→7). The wordmark behaves as if it lives inside the sphere.
- **Logomark → colour dot → circle-reveal to chapter card** (#7→9). The sphere recolours to brand azure and scales to fill. The brand colour literally comes out of the logo.
- **Chaos → order fly-in** (#12→14): scattered depth tiles converge into a column.
- **Cursor-click glow bloom** (#17→19): a radial blue gradient grows from the click point / corner to flood the frame into the next chapter card. The click *causes* the transition.
- **In-card content swaps** (#26→27, #30→31, #32→33): the card frame stays and only its body changes, with the button click as the motivation. No camera change, like a real wizard.
- **Blue card shrink → icon** (#39→40): the chapter card contracts (you briefly see an octagonal, heavily-rounded shape) into the agent icon. The inverse of #8→9.
- **Input expansion** (#42→43) and **zoom punch into the send button** (#45→46→48).
- **Result card shrink → logomark pop** (#56→57).
- The film is a **palindrome**: logo → blue → … → blue → logo, and every chapter entry and exit uses circle-scaling.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- #1–2: a pale horizontal streak behind the sphere (~0.5 frame width, very low contrast) is its entrance trail. Speed is conveyed by the trail, not by blur.
- #3 and #58: the "V" is literally hidden behind the sphere during the wipe. The mask edge is the sphere's circumference, so the letters appear to emerge from it.
- #8: the dot is azure, not gradient. The recolour happens during the scale-up, on the frame it detaches from the wordmark.
- #9–11 and #37–39: the white ellipses are rings of different radii and rotations, forming a loose wireframe globe. They rotate a few degrees per frame. #19–20 uses concentric circles instead, a per-chapter variant of the same motif.
- #12–13: the tile field has **three depth planes**. Back tiles are small and blurred ~6 px, mid tiles sharp, and some front tiles cropped by the frame edge and blurred.
- #16–18: in the macro, the non-selected neighbours (Controlling Agent, Legal Risk Assessor) are blurred ~8 px. Selection is shown with DOF, not a highlight colour.
- #18: the blue bloom starts at the bottom-right, where the cursor came from, not at the click point. The bloom continues the cursor's direction of travel.
- #22–24: **new text arrives tinted** (the most recent ~6 words are blue/pink gradient) and **fades to body grey** as the next words arrive. A "hot text" trail that reads as AI generation.
- #25–26: the cursor overlaps the Next button and the button fill becomes a blue gradient (hover and press in one).
- #28–30: the hover band on list rows is a light-grey rounded rect that **follows the cursor with a 1-frame lag**.
- #33: the cursor enters from the bottom edge before the file stack is visible. The file stack is drawn as a fanned pile (PDF, PDF, XLS) with small rotations.
- #35: when the files land, the Next button relabels to "Complete". The CTA copy changes to signal the end of the wizard.
- #39: the blue card collapses with **rounded-octagon corners**, not as a clean circle. It suggests a squircle-morph toward the icon shape.
- #46–47: the extreme macro on the send button is ~8× zoom with the cursor still at full size. The cursor scales with the camera and is enormous (~35% of frame).
- #50–52: "Reviewing contract…" has a shimmer. The left part of the text is brighter on successive frames (a left→right sheen over ~1.5 s).
- #54: the result card appears as a blurred ghost with only partial text ("Cont…") before resolving. A blur-in with the text revealed slightly late.
- Chapter-card text holds are short: ~1–1.5 s each.

## 6. Pacing curve
Very even, metronomic tempo: nearly every beat is 1–2 s. Logo intro (2.5 s) → chapter card (1.5 s) → tiles (3.5 s) → chapter card (1 s) → a wizard with four steps at 1–3 s each (instructions is longest because of typing) → chapter card (1.5 s) → the usage demo (7 s, the longest and slowest act, where the payoff lives) → a 1.5 s result hold → logo (2.5 s). The only real "punch" is the 8× zoom on the send button (#46), the kinetic climax right before the result. The ending mirrors the opening exactly for loopability.

## 7. What makes it premium
- **Strict system:** two backgrounds, one accent colour, one cursor, one card style. It looks designed as a style guide rather than as a set of scenes.
- The cursor is treated as a hero object: oversized, bevelled, brand-coloured. Every transition is triggered by its click (tile click → bloom, Next → swap, Complete → chapter, send → zoom).
- The logomark is the transition engine. It becomes the blue dot that becomes the chapter card that becomes the agent icon.
- DOF blur for selection instead of outlines or highlights.
- "Hot" typed text that cools to grey sells the AI writing without a spinner.
- A palindromic structure makes it loop cleanly as a website hero or social post.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Logo-as-mask wordmark.** Place the wordmark behind the logomark and reveal it with a left→right wipe whose leading edge is the mark's circumference (0.75 s, easeOutCubic). Reverse it to exit. Reuse the same animation at the end for a palindrome.
2. **Brand dot → circle reveal chapter card.** Recolour the logomark to the flat brand colour while scaling it 1→3 (0.5 s), then scale to cover the frame (0.3 s, easeInQuad). The chapter title sits on top with slowly rotating 1 px outline ellipses at 20% opacity.
3. **Click-direction bloom.** On a cursor click, emit a radial gradient from the cursor's entry side that floods the frame in ~0.5 s and lands on the next chapter card.
4. **Wizard card with in-place body swaps.** One persistent modal (title left, pill CTA right). Each click swaps the body in ≤1 frame. The CTA label changes on the last step ("Next" → "Complete").
5. **Hot-text typing.** Newly typed tokens render in a blue→pink gradient and ease to body grey over ~0.5–1 s as more text arrives. Speed ~40–60 characters per 0.5 s.
6. **Depth-field selection.** Tiles live on 3 planes. To select, push in 2× on the target and blur neighbours 6–10 px. Avoid outline highlights.
7. **Hero cursor.** Cursor at ~8% of frame height, brand-coloured, with bevel and soft shadow. The hover band trails it by 1 frame. For the key action, zoom 6–8× so the cursor and button fill the frame, then snap back.
8. **Chapter-card-to-icon collapse.** Shrink the full-frame colour card as a rounded squircle into the product's icon (0.5 s). The next UI (input field) grows from under that icon.
