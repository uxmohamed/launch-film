# Crosscut F: OpenAI Brand, Apple "has a plan", Aurel, Figma Draw, iCloud+

The films cover two brand/identity films (OpenAI Brand, 110 s; Apple 2030, 97 s), one Apple service spot (iCloud+, 27 s) and two product teasers (Aurel, 25.5 s; Figma Draw, 26 s).

## 1. Patterns that recur across these films

- **The atom is a circle, and it bookends.**
  - OpenAI's dot becomes the caret, the disc, the swatch, the video mask and the logo seed.
  - iCloud's lobes become the spheres and then the cloud again.
  - Aurel's ✦ survives the Dock tray and becomes the end mark.
  - Apple's logo glyph is the anchor inside the sentence (#9–10, where letters swap around it) and the final frame.

  Four of five films open and close on the same object (OpenAI dot→wordmark; Apple logo→"has a plan"→logo; iCloud cloud→cloud; Aurel ✦ in the tray → ✦ Aurel).
- **The inline icon or logo as a word.** Apple's logo replaces the word "Apple" in every sentence. Aurel's 🚲 arrives before the word "bike". The iCloud phone sits in the gap of "Get more ___ storage". The manual has "inline icons as words". The new part is that the icon **precedes** its word, or **replaces** the brand name for the whole film.
- **Slot reels everywhere, in five variants:**
  - word (Aurel Learn/Simulate/Invest);
  - subtitle (iCloud #39);
  - style, where one word is re-rendered in a new brush (Figma #2–6);
  - model name with descending scale (OpenAI #87–95);
  - hand-drawn icon plus label (Apple #37–39).

  All run at 0.5 s per item.
- **Type-only runtime is large.** Apple is 100% type (plus 7 s of footage). OpenAI is about 70% type or geometry. Aurel is 50% type before any UI. Real UI appears in only two films (Aurel about 8 s, Figma about 20 s), and there only as fragments: a panel, a toolbar, a sidebar crop or an input.
- **Construction before result.** OpenAI (circles → glyph, points → wordmark, rosette → blossom) and Figma (handles and paths visible as the art changes) both show the scaffolding.
- **White or near-white grounds dominate** (OpenAI, Aurel, iCloud, Apple's grey). Colour lives in the objects, not the ground, except in Figma (a full-bleed colour field per artwork) and Apple (a semantic 3-ground flip).

## 2. Contradictions (opposite choices, and why)

| Axis | Choice A | Choice B | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cuts vs. continuous | Apple: about 100 hard cuts, ground flips every 1–6 s | iCloud: **zero** hard cuts; every change is a blur or particle handoff | Apple is a spoken argument, so the cut is punctuation. iCloud sells calm and seamlessness. |
| Emphasis by scale | OpenAI: glyphs at 100–110% h, a "4o" at 90% h | Apple: its biggest claim is a **whisper** ("(This is a big one)" at ~1.5% h, #140) | Contrast works in both directions; the whisper works because the body size never changes. |
| Ending | OpenAI, Apple, iCloud, Aurel: subtract to logo, hold 1.5–3.5 s | Figma Draw: **no logo**; ends on a pull-back into a website | A feature launch within a known brand can end on the use-case. The title card at the open did the branding. |
| Accent colour | Apple: one green, hardly ever off screen | OpenAI: black and white for 60% of the runtime, then a whole palette at once | OpenAI saves colour for its own chapter. Apple makes green its second voice. |
| Arrival state | iCloud: glow-cool (brand-blue bloom → black) | Aurel: blur-scatter with rotation on **exit**; plain entrances | Aurel invests in exits, iCloud in entrances. |
| Camera | Figma: trucks and pull-backs | Apple, OpenAI: camera locked for about 95% of the runtime | Figma's content is spatial art. The type films let the layout carry the motion. |

## 3. Numbers and ranges (at 2 fps, ±0.5 s)

- **Word cadence:**
  - Apple: 1 word per 0.5 s, steady for 97 s;
  - OpenAI prompt typing: about 4 characters/s;
  - Aurel: a phrase of 3–5 words every 1–1.5 s.
- **Slot or variant flips:** 0.5 s per item in all five films.
- **Body type size:** 2–2.5% h (OpenAI prompt, Aurel serif), 4.5–5% h (Apple, iCloud headlines), 1.5% h subtitles.
- **Shouts:** OpenAI glyphs 60–110% h; Apple "all" about 30% h; Aurel macro input about 50% h.
- **Benefit or chapter length:** iCloud 4–4.5 s per benefit; OpenAI chapters 4–11 s (photography is the longest at 11 s); Figma 1–4 s per tool (longest is progressive blur at 4 s).
- **End-card hold:** OpenAI 3.5 s, iCloud 3 s, Aurel 1.5 s + 1 s white, Apple 1.5 s logo + black. There is often a dark or black tail after the last card (Apple 11.5 s, iCloud 1.5 s).
- **Atom scale jumps:** OpenAI dot ×20 in 1 frame (#13→14); iCloud sphere burst radial over about 1 s; the Apple ink blob fills the frame in about 1 s (#83→85).
- **Density ramps:** OpenAI confetti 5 → 60 dots in 3 frames; OpenAI tile size halves per frame for 4 frames; Apple product-name web 4 → 24 labels in 1 frame; Apple forest from 3 trees to a full frame in 2 frames.
- **Depth-of-field blur:** iCloud background spheres 8–15 px, lens-pass sphere about 40 px, Aurel exit blur about 6–10 px.

## 4. vs. the manual

### (a) Techniques NOT in the manual

1. **Spec-sheet HUD frame** (OpenAI #14–160).
   - A persistent hairline frame: four corner ticks, a top-centre chapter label and a bottom-centre sub-label, all at about 0.6% h.
   - Only the label text changes per chapter. It anchors about 60 hard cuts and makes the film read as a living document.
   - Recipe: fade it in with the first chapter, remove it for full-bleed footage, and bring it back for layout.
2. **Proofreader insertion layer** (Apple #6–8, #45–48, #124, #175).
   - A second, hand-lettered accent voice edits the main sentence live.
   - Recipe: a caret (^) at the insertion point; the word is hand-written along a 5–10° upward slant, stroke by stroke over 2–3 frames; the main line never moves.
   - Companions: hand-drawn underlines drawn L→R in 1 frame, rays around a keyword, parentheses in the accent colour, and a checkbox ticked after the clause completes.
3. **Semantic ground flip** (Apple).
   - Three grounds mapped to rhetorical role (grey = statement, green = pledge, black = contradiction or aside), hard-cut on the clause.
   - This is not chapter coding, since it flips every 1–6 s.
4. **Letter-to-object sprout** (Apple #63–69).
   - Selected letters shrink to dots and are replaced by same-height sprouting icons. The icon multiplies until it becomes the next ground (about 3 s end to end).
   - It is a verb transition for "grow" or "recycle".
5. **Ink-blob flood** (Apple #83–85). An illustrated element (factory smoke) expands organically to become the next ground, a hand-drawn cousin of "dot grows into ground".
6. **Arrow typology per noun** (Apple #97–104). Nouns in a list are each delivered by 3–5 arrows from off-frame, and the arrow *style* encodes the noun: smooth, looping, or orthogonal for dis-assemblers.
7. **Whisper emphasis** (Apple #140). The biggest claim is set at about a third of body size, alone on the accent ground, for 1.5 s. It is the inverse of the scale shout.
8. **Glow-cool arrival state** (iCloud every headline). Text appears as a brand-colour bloom (15–25 px glow) and cools to solid black in 0.5–1 s with no positional motion. Add it as a sixth option to the manual's arrival-state list.
9. **Blur-scatter exit** (Aurel #3, #6, #26, #45). Per word: blur to about 10 px, lift 1–2%, random ±3° rotation, staggered L→R. It is an *exit* arrival state, which the manual never specifies.
10. **Survivor tray** (Aurel #10–13). A familiar dock of generic app icons slides and contracts symmetrically until only your icon remains, then the title names it.
11. **Logo shatter / reassemble** (iCloud #5→6, #46→49). The mark splits into its primitives, which become the content particles of every scene, and the exact reverse paths rebuild it at the end.
12. **Particle family per benefit** (iCloud). Keep the particle form and swap its content per benefit (photos → icon tokens → avatars).
13. **Self-restyling title** (Figma #2–6). The product name itself is the variant-flip subject, re-rendered in a new brush every 0.5 s.
14. **Draw-around anchor** (Figma #7–9). The UI element sits still and the output is drawn around it.
15. **Scale-descending slot reel** (OpenAI #89–95). Each hard-cut item is about 50% smaller than the last, so the reel decelerates into a dot.
16. **Exponential density tile** (OpenAI #57–61). Item size halves per frame until the set fills the frame, with one roving accent item.
17. **Outline-vs-fill glyph pair** (OpenAI #49–56). The same glyph at 100% h shown twice side by side, construction vs. final, with a hard cut per glyph.
18. **Painting ground plate** (Aurel). A single public-domain art series serves as both card art and the full-bleed backdrop behind the UI window.
19. **Footage-with-word slideshow** (Apple #54–59). Macro industrial footage, one word per 0.5 s cut, with the word overlaid in white at body size.
20. **Tracking-out URL exit** (Apple #191). Letter-spacing widens about 30%, then a cut to the logo at the same centre.

### (b) Contradictions with the manual

- **"Things become, they don't cut."** Apple has about 100 hard cuts, and continuity is carried only by the fixed centre-line position, the constant type size and the 3-ground rhythm. That is closer to the manual's B9 "hard cuts with a carrier", but at a scale the manual treats as an exception. OpenAI's colour chapter (#136–142) also hard-cuts the swatch *and its position* every second.
- **"Change one axis at a time between scenes, unless it's a chapter break."** Apple flips ground colour, text colour and wording together every 1–6 s (e.g. #12→13→15). It works because the type size and position never change.
- **"Scale shout 25–40% of frame, once per film."** Apple uses one shout ("all", about 30%, #137) *plus* a whisper as the actual emotional peak (#140). OpenAI has about 20 frames with glyphs at 60–110% h, so shouting is the chapter's texture, not a one-off.
- **"The atom appears in the logo; end by subtraction; hold the final card 2–5 s."** Figma Draw ends with no logo at all (#52 is the poster inside a website). Aurel's logo holds only 1.5 s. Apple's logo alone holds 1.5 s, then 11.5 s of black.
- **"Film staggers are 0.4–0.5 s, not UI speed."** Aurel's blur-scatter word stagger is about 1 frame (≤0.1 s at 2 fps sampling), close to UI speed, and it reads fine because it is an *exit*.
- **"Device-frame rule: a device only when the platform is the point."** iCloud uses a full iPhone with its bezel as the storage hero (#7–10) for Apple's own product. For platform owners, the device *is* their world.
- **"A persistent ground plate… never a void."** OpenAI, Aurel and iCloud all sit on flat white or grey for most of the runtime, with no texture, grid or aurora. The ground is only a "world" through the HUD (OpenAI) or the art (Aurel). The white void is fine when the type or particles are strong.
- **"Pace for reading: a line readable 1.5–2.5 s."** Apple replaces the line every 0.5–1 s, and each on-screen fragment is 1–3 words. It is readable because the fragments are short. The rule should be about words per second (about 2), not line hold time.

### (c) Manual claims strongly confirmed

- **One brand atom as the transition engine:** OpenAI's dot carries more than 8 transitions (caret, disc, rings, grid, swatch, confetti, mask, logo seed), the strongest example in the library. iCloud's sphere carries all four benefits plus both logo moments.
- **Slot reel for lists, with a 25–40% preview:** Aurel #17 and #20 (next word at about 25% grey below), iCloud's subtitle roll #39, and OpenAI's "o1 2" outline preview.
- **Depth of field for selection:** iCloud #39–42 (one Memoji sharp and 1.3×, the others blurred about 10 px).
- **Push to affordance before the result:** Aurel #38 (the send button alone at about 15% h), then the answer.
- **Macro → context pull-back:** Aurel #33–35 (the caret text at 50% h, then the full input card), and Figma #45–52 (grain → poster → website).
- **Show the verb, not before/after:** Figma shows every tool mid-drag (blur slider 0→40 while the blur grows, texture spread 4→85 with visible grain growth, text sliding along its path).
- **Numbers and values tick with the effect:** Figma's slider values visibly change during #26–30 and #41–45.
- **Bookend the open, end by subtraction:** OpenAI (dense collage → hard cut to a lone blossom → wordmark), Apple (sentence → URL → logo), iCloud (particles → cloud).
- **Vary the mechanism, not the tempo:** Apple keeps 0.5 s per word for 97 s and stays fresh only by rotating the mechanism: caret insert, doodle slot, footage slideshow, sprout, ink flood, arrows, glow.
