# OpenAI Brand (identity system film) — 221 frames ≈ 110 s

## 1. One-line concept
A brand-guidelines film: the ChatGPT "dot" is the single atom, and it spawns every chapter of the identity: principles, construction geometry, typeface, logo, colour, photography, layout. It ends as the wordmark. The spine is "one dot becomes a whole system", told as a spec document brought to life.

## 2. Visual world
- **Ground:** pure white for about 80% of the runtime. There is one polarity flip to black (#136). Full-bleed photography is used only in the photo chapter (#161–184).
- **Spec-sheet HUD:** throughout #14–#160 a tiny persistent frame surrounds the stage. It has a top-centre chapter label, a bottom-centre sub-label, and tick marks in the four corners. All of it is at about 0.6% of frame height and unreadable at a glance. It turns every shot into a page of a manual. It disappears for photography and returns for layout.
- **Type:** OpenAI Sans, a geometric grotesk. There are three registers:
  - body at about 2% of frame height (the prompt "What can I help with?", the principle list);
  - display glyphs at 60–110% of frame height (#49–56, #63–68, the 0/1 at #75), cropped by the frame edge;
  - the wordmark at about 7%.
- **Palette:** black on white until #133. Then the brand colour set is shown as swatches (royal blue, sky, lavender, electric blue, navy, black, white), then a confetti of an extended set that adds yellow, coral, pale green and pink.
- **Imagery:** hairline construction geometry, such as circles, tangent circles, Bézier handles and metric boxes (#37–48, #100–110, #118–126). Warm golden-hour nature footage (sea spray, sea stacks, silhouettes). A final editorial collage of posters and papers (#197–210).
- **Depth:** none. There are no shadows. Everything is flat and orthographic, and depth comes only from overlap in the collage.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Dot → everything (atom scale jump):** #13→14, #96, #116, #130. The dot grows to a disc in a single frame. The cause is always "the next chapter starts here".
- **Dot as caret:** #5–11. The atom stands in for the ChatGPT typing cursor, which makes it the product's own verb.
- **Front-deletion:** #11→12. The prompt is erased from the start, not the end, so the dot is left alone at its original spot.
- **Chaos → grid snap:** #22→23. A random scatter locks into a radial matrix in one frame.
- **Push into construction:** #37→44. A slow zoom into Bézier arcs turns geometry into letterform.
- **Exponential tile shrink:** #57→61. The glyph size halves per frame (a fill-the-frame density ramp).
- **Polarity flip:** #135→136. Black disc on white becomes a white disc on black, a colour hard cut that carries the geometry.
- **Swatch hop:** #137–141. The same disc cuts to a new colour *and* a new position each second, so the jump reads as rhythm, not as a cut.
- **Confetti converge:** #153→155. Sixty dots collapse into one.
- **Dot as window:** #155→156→161. The atom becomes a circular video mask, then expands to full frame.
- **Aspect-morph box:** #186→191. A logo clear-space box becomes a poster frame and then a grid, a lineage morph done with an empty container.
- **Max-density → single mark:** #210→211. A hard cut from the densest collage to a lone blossom (subtraction by cut).

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- **The HUD labels change per chapter** while the corner ticks never move (#14–160). It is a fixed anchor across about 60 hard cuts.
- The **principle-list bullet** is the same dot as the atom. It sits left of the active word and steps down the list (#25 Simplicity, #26 Space, #30 Imperfection, #31–34 Vivid).
- **Imperfection is literal:** the ring of dots at #30–31 is visibly irregular, with one dot pushed off the circle.
- The **roving highlight glyph** in the character set (orange Å/Ė/Ł/Ø at #58–60, blue at #61–62) jumps each frame, as if an unseen cursor were inspecting the set.
- **Model reel scale ladder:** each model name is smaller than the last (4o at 90% h → o3 45% → GPT-2 12% → o1 5%), so the reel decelerates visually into a dot.
- In "o1 2" (#94) the "2" is an outline ghost. It works as a slot-reel preview state rendered as an outline instead of opacity.
- **Photography captions** are not a sentence line. Each word is placed on its own step of a diagonal staircase (#161–165, #175–178) at about 1.2% h. The type becomes topography over the landscape.
- **Swatch label inside the disc** (#133–142) is 0.5% h. It is legible only on pause and rewards the second watch.
- **Blossom construction:** a six-circle rosette (#119–120) precedes the knot. The logo's geometry is proven before it is shown.

## 6. Pacing curve
- A quiet open: 2 s of dot, then 3.5 s of typing.
- A steady 1–1.5 s cadence through principles and type (#14–76), then acceleration to 0.5 s hard cuts in the kinetic type and the model reel (#82–95). That is the first peak.
- A breath of logo construction (#96–129, about 17 s, slow).
- Colour picks up to a 1 s cadence, then confetti.
- The longest breath is photography at about 11 s of slow drifting footage. It is the emotional exhale and the only shot with a human.
- The layout grid is calm. The collage density ramps to a second, bigger peak (#197–210).
- Hard cut to the blossom for 2 s, then the wordmark holds 3.5 s on white. The ending subtracts by cut, not by fade.

## 7. What makes it premium
- One primitive (a dot) authors every chapter, including the photo chapter (it becomes the mask).
- The HUD makes it read as a living spec document, not a sizzle.
- Scale jumps are extreme (2% prompt → 110% glyph) yet the stage stays empty.
- Construction is shown before result three times (circles → glyph, points → wordmark, rosette → blossom).
- The kinetic-type burst is saved for the middle, so the rest can be calm.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Spec-sheet HUD frame.** Draw hairline corner ticks, a top-centre chapter label and a bottom-centre sub-label at about 0.6% h, kept for the whole film. Swap only the label text per chapter. This gives about 60 cuts a fixed anchor.
2. **Atom-as-caret prompt.** The brand dot sits where the cursor would be. Type about 2 characters per frame (≈4 chars/s) to its left and re-centre the line. Exit by deleting from the front so the dot is left in place.
3. **Principle re-weighting.** Keep one fixed dot matrix and express each abstract value by which dots are enlarged or coloured. Hold 1.5 s per value, with a bullet-dot cursor in a side list.
4. **Outline-vs-fill glyph pairs.** Show the same glyph at 100% h twice, outline on the left and filled on the right, with a hard cut per glyph at 1 s.
5. **Exponential density tile.** Halve the item size each frame for 4–5 frames until the set fills the frame. Add one roving accent item.
6. **Scale-descending slot reel.** Hard-cut item names every 0.5 s, each about 50% smaller than the last, so the reel lands softly on a small item.
7. **Dot → mask → full-bleed.** Play footage inside the atom disc for 2 s, then expand the mask to the frame over 1–2 frames.
8. **Container-aspect morph.** A logo clear-space box resizes into the page frame and then the column grid. Keep the logo pinned at the top-centre while the box grows.
