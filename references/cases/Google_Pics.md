# Google Pics, analysis (198 frames, ~99 s)

## 1. One-line concept
The launch of Google Pics, a Gemini-powered image creation and editing app (pics.new). The spine is "Say hello to Google Pics: create anything you imagine". It is followed by a feature list of captions:
- Isolate objects / for precision editing
- Change elements instantly
- Edit text directly in images
- Localize with one click
- Integrations with Workspace
- Share for easy collaboration
- Total control over AI image creation

Each caption is followed by a real prompt → generate → result loop. It ends on a carousel of outputs and "Try it now at pics.new".

## 2. Visual world
- **Ground:** pure white with a **blue-violet edge glow**: a soft gradient vignette bleeding in from the left and bottom edges (~15–20% of the frame), static apart from a slight breathing. Every caption card and every UI wide sits on it.
- **Type:** Google Sans (a geometric grotesk), regular weight, black. Captions are ≈4–6% of frame height and two lines, centred; the end URL is ≈10% of frame height. The one accent is Gemini blue (#4285f4-ish), used on the ghost words, highlights, the marker circle and the send button.
- **UI:**
  - The real dark Pics app window at ~70% of frame width with a thin border (#28–38, #48, #98, #143, #166).
  - A **prompt box pulled forward** over the dimmed app, which sits under a ~60% grey/white wash. The box is ~85% width, dark #1f1f1f, white text (#16–18, #91–94, #118–122, #156–162, #170–174).
  - Macro 3–4× crops of the send button (#19–22) and the toolbar (#144–148).
  - A dark **status-pill macro** ("Thinking…", "Generating image…") with a big blue radial bloom (#23–27, #126–128).
- **Cursor:** an oversized stylised white pointing-hand cursor (~6–8% of frame height) with a blue offset shadow. It clicks UI **and also clicks the captions themselves**.
- **Imagery:** generated posters (flat, colourful, risograph-like) and product photos. This is the product output, which gives the film its colour against the monochrome UI.

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Colour bridge (status → canvas):** the blue bloom of the "Generating" macro continues as the blue gradient placeholder inside the app canvas (#27→28, #128→129, #165→166). The AI's colour carries the cut.
- **Macro send → cut to status:** the click is the cause (5×).
- **Caption click → caption performs the feature** (#40–42, #59–62, #87–88, #151–152). The transition *into* the demo is the caption turning into a UI object first.
- **Blur dissolve** between caption lines and out of cards (#11, #90, #107–108).
- **Icon portal:** the app icon is clicked and the app materialises behind it (#112–113).
- **Carousel → pile → URL** (#187–193).

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- **The ritual never varies:** dim the app → prompt box → paragraph-at-once or typewriter text → push 3.5× to the send button → the cursor enters from the lower right → click → dark status pill → a pull-back to the placeholder. Five repetitions, ~7–10 s each.
- The prompt box has its own soft shadow and sits *in front of* the dimmed app (~60% wash). That's a two-plane staging of a real app.
- The placeholder aspect ratio matches the output (portrait for flyers, landscape for photos) before the image resolves.
- In "Change elements instantly" only one word is ghosted. Clicking it resolves it, so the arrival state becomes an interaction.
- The two collaborator cursors are different hand shapes (open palm, pointing) and drift independently: fake multiplayer on a caption.
- The marker ellipse is hand-drawn (uneven stroke) and stays on screen only ~1 s.
- "Try it now at": the late word "at" arrives offset up and to the right, then settles (#193→194), which is the manual's late-tail pattern. The lockup then shrinks rather than pushes.
- The legal disclaimer line stays at the bottom of every UI shot at ~1% of frame height. It's a real launch constraint, handled as texture.

## 6. Pacing curve
- Open: 7 s (hello → collage → thesis).
- Then 7 caption/demo pairs of ~10–14 s each: caption 2–4.5 s, demo 4–10 s.
- The rhythm is deliberately uniform, like a tutorial.
- The densest demo is Workspace (#112–137, ~13 s continuous).
- Breaths: every caption card on white.
- The climax is the carousel sweep (#187–190), the only 3D and fast move in the film. It ends on the URL (~3 s).

## 7. What makes it premium
- The captions are not labels: each one is an **interactive object** that demonstrates its own feature before the UI does.
- There's one accent colour (Gemini blue) and it means one thing, the AI. It links the ghost words, the highlights, the status bloom and the placeholder.
- The real UI is shown honestly but staged in two planes (dimmed app plus a forward prompt) with macro send beats. It reads as designed, not recorded.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Caption performs the feature.** The cursor clicks a word in the caption and the word undergoes the feature (selected/boxed, recoloured, translated, circled) within 0.5–1 s. Then cut to the UI doing the same.
2. **Prompt ritual.**
   - Dim the app to 40% under a white wash.
   - Pull the prompt box forward (85% width, soft shadow).
   - Text appears; push 3–4× to the send button (0.5 s, ease-in-out); the cursor clicks.
   - Cut to the status macro (1.5–2.5 s).
   - Pull back to a placeholder in the output's aspect ratio (1 s); the result resolves.
3. **Status bloom colour bridge.** Make the "thinking" shot's radial glow exactly the colour and position of the placeholder gradient in the next wide, so the cut reads as continuous.
4. **Caption swap with fixed satellites.** The caption blurs out and in (0.5 s) while the surrounding icons keep their positions and drift slowly.
5. **Icon portal.** An app icon (10% height) is clicked and the app UI fades up behind it (0.5 s) as the icon dissolves into its place.
6. **Carousel → pile → URL finale.** Outputs sweep on a curved plane (1.5 s, linear, with parallax), converge to a pile (0.5 s), and the pile shrinks as the URL blurs in.
7. **Hand-drawn marker emphasis.** Draw a blue ellipse around the key noun in ~0.5 s, hold 0.5 s, and remove it.
