# Superpower (superpower.com): consumer health / blood-test platform launch (169 frames ≈ 85 s)


## 1. One-line concept
Superpower sells a 100+ marker blood test that feeds a "Digital Twin" of your body plus an AI you can ask. Spine: **two-world film** — a hot, analog, orange editorial-collage "body" world (mystery, anxiety: "Is this normal? / Why am I so tired? / Should I be worried about this?") that is *resolved* into a cool, white, clinical-clean product world ("Your body is a miracle … understandable … yours. … Control your health is finally yours … Know how you really stand … The story of your health. Alive. Changing. Uniquely yours."). The film is literally a before/after of the viewer's *state of mind*, with the wordmark as the hinge between the two worlds (#59–63).

## 2. Visual world
- **Two grounds, hard-coded by act:**
  - Act I (#1–61): photographic, tinted to a monochrome orange/amber duotone (shadows brown-black, highlights cream). Macro skin, eyelids (#1–2), dancers' backs and arms (#3–10), faces sliced into vertical strips (#21–22). Heavy film grain, halation, horizontal light-streak smears (#12–18).
  - Act I-b (#29–58): black ground with sparse white data glyphs (tiny bars, tick ladders, dot rows) and a thin pale ellipse; orange imagery lives only inside a frame/lozenge.
  - Act II (#62–158): pure white/very light grey ground, thin 1px light-grey construction lines (a cross/grid of 4 lines forming a central cell, #62–63, #72–73, #128–129), large faint circle arcs, a dot grid (#83–90). Frosted white 3D mannequins (Digital Twin) with a soft mint-green glow blob as the only colour (#69–71, #91–96, #141–144).
  - Reprise (#136–140, #159–162) returns to orange collage and orange silk macro before the white end card.
- **Palette:** Act I = orange #F08A1E / cream / brown-black, occasional acid green-yellow fringing (#11–20). Act II = white, 20% grey hairlines, mint #6EE7B7 glow (health "hot spot"), product-orange for the tube cap and send-arrow (#74–82, #123–127), one forest-green card (Biological age, #120–122).
- **Type:** neutral grotesk (Inter/Aeonik-like), regular weight, sentence case. Captions small: Act I "Your body is a miracle" ≈ 1.8% of frame height (#8); Act I questions ≈ 3.5% (#36–48); Act II headlines ≈ 4.5–5% ("understandable", "Control your health"); the shout is the wordmark "SU" outline at ~55% frame height (#59–61). Wordmark in its own rounded geometric face.
- **Collage vocabulary (Act I):** vertical stripe slices of different photos side by side, rectangles of flat orange, halftone dot patches, pixel-stepped black silhouettes (8-bit staircase edges, #23–28), a huge thin circle outline overlaid on everything (#7–22), small "+" crosshairs and HUD registration boxes (#21, #29–34, #49–58).
- **UI staging (Act II):** the web app shown as a faint, low-contrast full window (Twin page, #65–68) sitting in white; then isolated components: a sidebar list (#91–96), score cards (#118–122), chat input pill (#123–127), "Upload" button (#128–129), file chips (#130–132), an LDL chart card (#133–135). One iPad-style device shot with a floating stylus (#97–100).

## 4. Transition inventory
- **Eye-open open (#1–2)** and **eye bookend (#145–147)** — a literal "open your eyes" framing device; the eye returns B/W and shrinks into a card.
- **Tint flood / double exposure (#2→#5)** — layered dissolve, colour warms from cool to orange across 3 frames.
- **Collage re-shuffle hard cuts (#7–28)** — every 0.5 s a new arrangement of stripes; continuity from the persistent thin circle outline and palette. Stop-motion cadence on black silhouettes.
- **Window → blurred lozenge (#34→#35)** — the rectangular image window collapses into a soft-edged ellipse (defocus ~60–80 px) that becomes the question stage.
- **Outline-glyph truck (#58→#61)** — the vector outline of the wordmark (anchors visible) sweeps in over the last Act I image, fills with the gradient, then whites out. This is the hinge between worlds: *design construction lines* literally carry Act I into Act II's construction-line grid.
- **Wordmark → construction cell (#61→#62)** — the giant letters shrink to "sup" inside the grid cell and complete typing.
- **Fade-up from white (#63→#65)** — UI arrives as a ghost, not a card.
- **Inline object in sentence (#77)** — the tube sits between "single" and "blood test", then **rotates 90° and scales ~3× to become the next shot's hero (#78)**: object carries over and re-orients.
- **Selection ↔ glow link (#91–96)** — list selection drives the 3D body's glow location: a UI state change transitions the 3D world.
- **UI → device pull-back (#96→#97)**.
- **Hard-cut reprise into orange collage (#135→#136)** — deliberately jarring return to Act I world before the twin.
- **Letter-mask reveal (#162)** — oversized wordmark letters as windows onto the silk footage; then collapses to the end card.
- **Colour-drain end (#163→#166)** — wordmark orange gradient → grey → black, i.e., end by subtraction of colour.

## 5. Easy-to-miss details
- **Legibility is deliberately sacrificed on the anxious word**: in the questions (#37, #41, #45) the key word ("normal", "so", "worried") sits over the brightest blob area and nearly disappears — the anxiety word is visually *blurred/uncertain*. Once in Act II, text is always dark-on-white and crisp.
- The blurred lozenge's inner colour field shifts per question (orange/pink → white-hot right side #40–43 → orange/pink again), ~1 s colour cycle, while the text holds.
- Dotted horizontal line runs through the lozenge like a scan line; small "+" marks sit above and below at the vertical centre line (#35–48). These crosshairs recur as the Act II construction cross (#62) — the same atom in two worlds.
- The HUD tracking boxes on the dancer (#49–58) move with her hand across ~10 frames — face-tracking graphics imply "measurement" of the body before the product is named.
- The wordmark is first shown as *outline with bezier anchor points* (#59–60) — "designed/constructed" — then filled.
- Act II headlines move vertical position between clauses: "Control your health" at upper third (#101–104) → "is finally yours." at centre (#106–108). The sentence walks down the frame.
- "is finally yours" → "yours." : the period arrives one frame after the word (#106→#107), a late-tail on punctuation.
- Number ticking in words, not a counter: "over 89 → over 99 → over 100 markers," — only 3 steps, each 0.5 s, and the comma after "markers" signals the sentence continues on the next shot.
- Digital Twin glow is always mint, never orange: orange = the unknown body (Act I, tube cap, CTA arrows), mint = "understood" state.
- Condition list (#109–117) fades rows toward top/bottom to ~10% (a soft vertical vignette), active row pure black with an em-dash marker; the list is the slot-reel pattern at 9+ rows.
- The chat pill jumps horizontally between three questions (#123 centre-right, #124 right, #126 left) — three different users implied without showing people.
- In the staccato ending (#151–158) a small square registration box leads each word and slides to the new word's start; the box is empty — it is the "twin"/scan frame reduced to an icon.
- End card: ".com" appends to the wordmark and the orange fill drains to grey then near-black in ~1 s (#163→#166).

## 6. Pacing curve
- 0–30 s (Act I): dense, texture-heavy, cuts every 0.5 s (#7–28), then **the long 7 s question breath** (#35–48) with very little motion — the slowest passage in the film sits inside the "anxious" act.
- 30–31 s: the SU outline truck — the one shout (fastest camera move).
- 31–50 s: calm white world, 1–2.5 s per beat, type-led.
- 50–68 s: product proof at a steady 0.5 s-per-step cadence (list steps, score ticks, questions, chip stack).
- 68–72 s: a short orange reprise (tension reminder).
- 72–80 s: "The story of your health… Alive. Changing. Uniquely yours." — staccato 1 s words.
- 80–85 s: silk → wordmark mask → colour drains → URL hold ~2.5 s.

## 7. What makes it premium
- The product world is *earned*: 30 s of editorial, fashion-film collage before a single UI pixel. Clean white UI reads as relief because the viewer has been in hot, noisy orange.
- Colour is semantic across the whole film: orange = the body/unknown, mint = understood, white = clarity.
- UI appears ghosted (#64–68), never as a hard-edged screenshot; every real component is isolated at a single-component scale on white.
- The construction line/crosshair atom crosses worlds (HUD cross in Act I → grid cell in Act II → registration box in the ending).
- The 3D mannequin is frosted, not realistic — clinical but warm, and the glow moves with UI selection.

## 8. Reusable techniques
1. **Two-world film (problem world vs. product world).** Act I: full-bleed textured duotone footage/collage, 0.5 s cuts, questions typed in the viewer's voice. Act II: white ground + hairline grid, 1–2.5 s beats. Hinge: the wordmark. Ratio ≈ 35:65.
2. **Outline-to-fill wordmark hinge.** Draw the logo letters as 1–2 px outlines with visible anchor points at ~50% frame height, truck across over the last problem-world shot (~1 s), fill with brand gradient (0.3 s), white-out, then re-type small "sup→superpower" inside a construction cell.
3. **Anxious-word dropout.** Place the question over a soft blob whose brightest region swallows the key word (low contrast), and cycle the blob's hue per question (~1 s). The uncertainty is typographic.
4. **Inline product object in the sentence, then carry-over.** A product (tube) at cap height between words ("A single [tube] blood test"); next shot rotates it 90° and scales ~3× to be the backdrop for the following clause.
5. **Selection-driven 3D glow.** Sidebar list (steps 1 row / 0.5 s) is linked to a soft radial glow moving across a frosted 3D body; UI state = world state.
6. **Condition slot reel with image pairing.** 9–12 row list, active row black + dash, edges fade to 10%; left image swaps per row. 0.5 s per step.
7. **Registration-box word lead.** A small empty square precedes each staccato word ("Alive." "Changing." "Uniquely."), sliding to the new word start between beats.
8. **Colour-drain URL.** End wordmark in brand gradient → grey → black over 1 s; append ".com"; hold 2.5 s.
